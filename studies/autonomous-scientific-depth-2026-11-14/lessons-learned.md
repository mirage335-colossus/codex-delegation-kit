# Lessons from the autonomous research exercises

The user ended additional exercise campaigning because the time and effort had
become disproportionate, then explicitly rejected historical replay as a reason
to retain bulky data. The [retention boundary](RETENTION.md) explains this reduced
collection. Original grades remain historical observations with their original
limits; deleted source payloads and raw histories cannot be re-audited here.

## Useful behavior and limits

The exercises demonstrated useful autonomous initiation, substantial inquiry,
independent criticism and concrete same-owner development uptake within the
configured scientific environments. The [V6](evaluation/primary-scientific-v6/operation-02/result.md),
[V7](evaluation/primary-scientific-v7/operation-01/result.md) and
[V8](evaluation/primary-scientific-v8/operation-01/result.md) records distinguish
successful uptake from incomplete science: all eight domains remained Partial,
with 20/48, 28/48 and 25/48 Supported depth judgments. These dependent episodes
establish neither causal improvement nor regression.

Reference-level depth and reliability remain unproven. The
[reference calibration](evaluation/reference-self-test/result.md) itself reached
seven complete domains and 47/48 adjudicated depth judgments, with historical
science separated from current-source obligations. It is not an autonomous
success or a substitute passing threshold. Automatic appropriate selection in
ordinary installed-app chats, cost savings and product-performance gains were
not established.

Retain scientific knowledge as a reusable work product; a dedicated native
research responsibility; the full engineering context; primary-source, contrary-
evidence and quantitative checks; independent critique; and explicit research-to-
development uptake. The [V9 source review](evaluation/development-v9/source-review.md)
accepted the dedicated role while preserving existing model settings and installer
compatibility. Its reported source checks establish software contracts, not
scientific attainment. The [source closeout](evaluation/development-v9/source-closeout.json)
records the final README correction and its narrower validation.

## Why repeated prompt revisions were insufficient

The [strategic diagnosis](evaluation/strategic-diagnosis.md) identifies a plausible
narrowing mechanism: research can become organized around defending a provisional
engineering plan instead of building explanatory knowledge that reveals additional
choices. This is a hypothesis informed by observed omissions, not a demonstrated
cause. The full-problem brief, independent inquiry, protected science review and
reconciliation variants produced useful behavior without meeting the full bar.

The [matched diagnostic synthesis](evaluation/matched-review/assessment-execution/diagnostic-synthesis.md)
and [experimental redesign](evaluation/experimental-redesign-2026-11-15.md)
preserve why further similar campaigns were not justified. A stronger future
experiment should resolve a specific uncertainty and separate scientific adequacy,
workflow activation, uptake, access quality and outcome validity. More citations,
more review stages or a larger allowance do not establish comprehensive science.

## The last pair is not a clean scientific contrast

The ordinary-development case completed after about 91.90 minutes. The research
case stopped at a runtime cutoff after about 129.39 minutes without a first root
terminal. The [qualitative closeout](evaluation/groundwave-matched-v9/review/retained-work-qualitative-closeout.md)
inspected 14 retained-at-the-time files and traced two development decisions.
It found useful source grounding, independent correction, calculations tied to
decisions and explicit limitations. It introduced no rubric scores, lower
threshold, causal comparison or new passing claim. The underlying scientific
work was unfinished; the raw archives are no longer bundled.

The [runtime diagnosis](evaluation/groundwave-matched-v9/review/r-case-runtime-hold-diagnosis.md)
records a native web-item start with an empty query, null action/results and no
matching completion. Acquisition, cost and delivery remained unknown. The driver
requested live web access while instructions required a mailbox, and the guard
stopped on native start. This exposes a driver/admission mismatch, not a completed
bypass or evidence of inability to research. Local cleanup did not prove
provider-side cancellation.

## Practical lessons for future improvement

- Match configured tool exposure, permitted access and runtime admission. A
  mailbox instruction cannot disable a separately exposed native route. A
  start-event guard does not prove that requests were metered or prevented before
  execution. The existing backend's disabled-web route is a reference to inspect
  before adapting a future harness.
- Use actual tool declarations and returned metadata. Validate reference shapes
  and timeout semantics. An outer timeout is not proof of inner cancellation.
  D6's routing stall lasted about 82 minutes and confounded interpretation;
  its [integrity report](evaluation/development-d6/integrity-review.md) retains
  the relevant delivery limitations.
- Include queue and service waiting in elapsed opportunity. Shared resource
  ceilings do not guarantee equal access or latency. The
  [service-readiness report](evaluation/groundwave-matched-v9/service-readiness.md)
  records relay waiting and unverified browser/image delivery.
- Keep first outcomes, later corrections, delivery integrity, scientific depth
  and uptake separate. Independent evaluator work cannot fill an author's gaps.
  A role label or internal acceptance is not proof of adequate science.
- Preserve the full research question through specialist and engineering
  boundaries. Keep contrary results, quantities, conventions and unresolved
  distinctions; do not equate readiness of one useful increment with coverage of
  the requested operating envelope.
- Prefer one decision-changing, bounded check to another broad campaign.
  Repeated incomplete science did not justify indefinite prompting, expanded
  resource use or relaxed acceptance. This cleanup launches no new experiment.
- Preserve compact authored reasoning, prompt variants, failures and implementation
  lessons. Acquired publications, recordings, spectra and repeated event/diff
  snapshots are not needed to retain these lessons. Avoid embedding external
  payloads in logs that will be redistributed.
