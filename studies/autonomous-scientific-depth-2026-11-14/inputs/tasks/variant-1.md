# Development request

Propose a phased design for a repeatable native bench pilot that separates channel assumptions, capture or front-end limitations and receiver limitations in demanding weak-signal operation. Cover sub-9 kHz electrically small antennas and practical coupling choices, groundwave near both 1 and 30 MHz including land and mixed coastal routes, 1–30 MHz skywave from quiet to disturbed and high-latitude conditions, and passive EME near 5.8 GHz. Decide which environmental effects need distinct representation and which existing controls are sufficient. In each range address received levels, delay and frequency behavior, polarization where relevant, noise, interference, front-end limitations and severe or absent operation. Identify the native interfaces and complete-receiver checks needed to choose what to build first. Preserve one continuous native path, public hypotheses, calendar behavior and demanding-case service capacity. Deliver an implementation-ready plan.
