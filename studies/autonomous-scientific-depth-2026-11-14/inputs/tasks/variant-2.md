# Development request

Select a practical staged design for the channel appraisal and receiver operating profiles that engineers would use when moving between sub-9 kHz electrically small antenna or alternative coupling deployments, groundwave near 1 and 30 MHz on land and mixed coastal routes, quiet through disturbed and high-latitude 1–30 MHz skywave, and passive EME near 5.8 GHz. Compare levels, timing and frequency behavior, polarization where relevant, noise and interference, real front-end limits, outages and defensible adverse cases. State which profile differences materially change observation, search or recovery, what can share the current implementation, and how to distinguish a real improvement from a scenario or instrument artifact. Preserve native continuity, public hypotheses and calendar behavior, and demanding-case complete-receiver capacity. Deliver an implementation-ready priority and validation plan.
