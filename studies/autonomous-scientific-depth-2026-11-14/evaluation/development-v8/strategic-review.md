## Existing methods and contrary evidence

I reused the verified STORM/AutoSurvey analysis in the earlier [mechanism review](../primary-scientific-v6/operation-02/diagnosis-preparation/astra-mechanism-review.md), then examined additional primary methods and limitations. No paper is evidence that this kit works.

* [FS-Researcher, ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.288.pdf), §§2–4 and limitations, separates evidence accumulation from section-wise writing in a persistent workspace. On ten sampled queries with GPT-5, reported RACE scores fall from 52.76 to 42.41 without the dual-agent separation and to 47.63 without section-wise writing. This supports investigating work/context separation. It does not isolate our proposed mechanism: the merged agent runs three rounds while the separate writer has no round limit; section-wise writing also changes sessions. The main report benchmarks use model judges. The authors acknowledge premature stopping and model dependence. V7 already stores source bytes and substantial reusable files, so simply adding a filesystem or more notes is not a materially new treatment. Import the testable separation idea, not unlimited writing, fixed round counts, or the paper's success claims.
* [MindSearch, inspected 2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.20183v1), §§2–3, uses evolving subquestions and separate retrieval contexts. Its authors specifically report that narrow subqueries can lose useful context; they pass the root question and relevant upstream response. Its open-question evaluation uses five human raters over 100 queries and reports no factuality improvement. This supports retaining the whole objective and relevant context in focused assignments, not fragmenting science into disconnected fact lookups or assuming decomposition ensures correctness.
* [DeepResearch Bench II, 2026 preprint](https://agentresearchlab.com/assets/papers/DeepResearch-Bench-II.pdf), §§3,5,7, separates information recall, analysis and presentation using expert-report-derived rubrics. It identifies both source leakage and subjective annotation limits, including the difficulty of satisfying every reviewer in one pass. This supports separating polished output from scientific attainment and preserving comparator-contamination controls. Its percentage scoring, tasks, models and judgments differ from this study's conjunction; its results neither validate our exact bar nor establish a capability ceiling.
* [STORM](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.347.pdf) gives relevant evidence for source-grounded evolving questions, with weaker human coverage significance and no verifiability improvement in the previously inspected evaluation. [AutoSurvey](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/d07a9fc7da2e2ec0574c38d5f504d105-Paper-Conference.pdf) supports subject-level retrieval/drafting as an available approach; its small aggregate content difference with reflection and evaluator limitations do not justify another critic loop as the principal repair.

Fresh literature does not justify changing model pins, installing an external research framework, borrowing hidden domain rubrics, or promising that more sessions will attain parity.
