## The P.372 claim and the empirical gap

The city/rural coefficients reproduce −97.175/−106.775 dBm/Hz at 1 MHz and −138.091/−147.691 dBm/Hz at 30 MHz after adding the 290 K thermal reference. The recommendation defines this as the outdoor AWGN component at the short, vertical, lossless grounded-monopole reference. The city/rural interval is 0.3–250 MHz. The quiet-rural and Galactic cases have their own ionospheric limits. The author explicitly identifies the 2024 edition and preserves the failed 2026-edition PDF route. I confirmed the 2026 listing and size failure, and found no adoption of an uninspected newer curve. I also checked Part 7. Those are distinct calculations. This has value for deciding how to treat a measured depression of cosmic noise and the limits of applying an absorption forecast to arrival/availability. The measurement is a relative absorption observable from one selected flare case. It does not supply a site noise floor or a comparison with the P.372 environmental medians.
