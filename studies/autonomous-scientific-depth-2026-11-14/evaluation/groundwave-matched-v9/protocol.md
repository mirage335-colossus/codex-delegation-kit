## Question and claim scope

Can the fixed accepted V9 candidate attain the original reference-level
groundwave comparison when explicitly commissioned to produce reusable science,
and when given an ordinary development task for the same groundwave problem?
For the ordinary arm, does it appropriately initiate and sustain the necessary
inquiry and use the resulting knowledge in the requested plan without root
scientific prompting?

GROUND is deliberately selected as a demanding development case: the reference
self-test meets all six depth checks, while PC4 met two and V8 one. Those
historical results are descriptive and not matched treatment estimates. V8's
eight Partial domains and 25/48 Supported depth judgments, V7's 28/48, and their
useful uptake remain unchanged. The qualified reference calibration remains
47/48 overall after adjudication; it does not replace the original acceptance
rule with a new threshold. The earlier matched-review contrast remains
inconclusive after material transport asymmetry.

A groundwave pass establishes component feasibility under this selected case
and opportunity. It does not establish all-domain breadth, reliability,
installed-app selection, or a fundamental capability limit if it fails. The
contrast tests the whole assignment/workflow response, including any resulting
staffing, prioritization and closure choices. It cannot isolate wording, the
new role, context isolation, model choice or a cost advantage.
