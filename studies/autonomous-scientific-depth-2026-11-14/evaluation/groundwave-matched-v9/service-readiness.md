### Existing asynchronous alternative and its limits

The V8 web/public scripts do support concurrent `Promise.allSettled` invocation within a functions cell, but publication happens only after all calls settle. V7's operator-web-service-window.js (historical locator: `../primary-scientific-v7/operation-01/execution/operator-web-service-window.js`; artifact not bundled) polls, then awaits that same whole batch. Neither is already a responsive two-mailbox service. A shared pair-only driver would need independent case slots, per-result publication, root-review handoff and live polling while calls remain pending. `functions.exec` early yield/`yield_control()` and waits of at most 60 seconds are supported primitives, but yielding alone does not establish second-mailbox service; unawaited promises are discarded when the cell ends. Do not assume cross-cell promise transfer or cancellation from a JavaScript timeout race. With root adopting two separately bound operators, this new driver is unnecessary.
