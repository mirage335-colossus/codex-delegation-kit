## The sole assigned procedural difference

**Arm G — full-scope generalist review.** Each Sol reviewer reviews the entire
scientific account against the original full brief, applying the existing V6
scientific review provisions. Each independently examines material questions,
alternatives, source support and feasible quantitative checks. The Astra lead
reconciles both full reviews, undertakes its own full-scope assessment and asks
for scientifically warranted follow-through. Do not prescribe a specialist
partition or the treatment's argument-reconstruction procedure.
Natural argument reconstruction or equivalent reasoning under V6 is permitted
and recorded; the control is not instructed to avoid sound scientific work.

**Arm C — complementary scientific ownership.** From the three independently
generated initial scopes, the Astra lead assigns the two Sol reviewers
complementary scientific responsibilities that together cover the original
problem. Choose the boundaries from the task and discovered explanatory
questions, not from a supplied domain roster, software modules or hidden
criteria. Both reviewers retain the full brief. The lead explicitly retains
connections, overlaps and questions not owned by either responsibility.

Each assigned reviewer reconstructs the material arguments in its responsibility:
what observations and measurement conditions support an explanation, which
competing explanations remain possible, and what inference or decision the
evidence warrants. It attempts a source-based or numerical discriminator for a
consequential unsupported connection where feasible, and owns the resulting
argument through final disposition rather than merely forwarding papers. A
compact dependency diagram is optional; the work is reconstructing and testing
the argument, not drawing a graph. Findings that expand the problem or cross a
responsibility boundary return to the lead for explicit ownership and review.
The lead reconciles the whole account and the coverage of those connections.

This bundles complementary allocation with a concrete reading/verification
procedure. It does **not** isolate domain knowledge, a model effect, reviewer
count, role naming or any single sentence. Do not call these actors proven
domain specialists merely because they received responsibilities.
