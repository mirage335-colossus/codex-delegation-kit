# Coverage refinement after the first comparison

Before declaring the research sufficient, independently check its coverage against the original outcome and requested operating conditions, not only the chosen algorithm or the papers easiest to obtain. Consider relevant physical mechanisms, environment/regime, measurement definitions, implementation and operating practice, and validation. Use existing curated research as a coverage reference when supplied, without copying its conclusions as truth. For each consequential omitted branch, either investigate it, explain why it cannot change this task's decision, or retain a concrete unresolved prerequisite. Primary technical recommendations, measurement reports and documented operating evidence can complement academic papers where they fill such a gap; identify their different authority and transfer limits. Rank and resolve useful gaps within the remaining budget before repeating evidence for an already supported central recommendation.

This is a task-specific coverage check, not a universal checklist, paper quota or a claim of exhaustive systematic review. Do not broaden a settled small task. A bounded report with important open branches is a useful intermediate result, not evidence of full requested coverage.
