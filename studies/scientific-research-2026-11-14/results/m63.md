## Channel model

Fable already corrected its blanket claim of Lorentzian absence. The production simulator exposes `PathEvolutionConfig::ray_coherence_seconds`, with independent stationary unit-power proper-complex gains on the direct and delayed rays. The code initializes them in stationarity and advances them with decay `a = exp(-dt/τ)` and innovation standard deviation `sqrt((1-a²)/2)` per quadrature. Here `dt` includes the RX clock scale. It multiplies the rays before combining them and advances the state through source silence. Exponential covariance gives a Lorentzian continuous-time gain spectrum and an aliased discrete-time counterpart. This is the specified synthetic per-ray law. A measured non-Lorentzian spectrum, coupled scattering, or empirical calibration would be a distinct objective. Reuse the option for the inherited Lorentzian objective and defer channel-model changes pending a concrete gap.
