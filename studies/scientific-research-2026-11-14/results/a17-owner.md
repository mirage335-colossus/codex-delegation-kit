**What to represent.** Use `y(t)=Σ a_k(t) exp[-j2π f₀ τ_k(t)] x(t−τ_k(t))` on the whole shaped complex waveform, with measured/assumed diffuse gain or residual Doppler in `a_k` and separate oscillator and noise controls. Do not add a geometric frequency shift a second time: delay contributes `−f₀ dτ/dt` at RF and retimes the envelope. For locally uncorrelated taps, the same-time frequency covariance is proportional to `Σ P_k exp(−j2π Δf τ_k)`; lagged covariance also needs the per-path temporal law. Use these quantities across the actual Helper and DATA bands and lags. For example, default DATA spacing is 75 Hz (`triad_modem_v3.hpp:22–31`, `triad_modem_v3.cpp:138–140`); an illustrative equal-power 0/7 ms pair has adjacent `|ρ|≈.08` and outer-to-outer `|ρ|≈.99`. A pilot fade or one weakly correlated pair is poor evidence for independent gains. Narrow pulse bandwidth with negligible phase variation across each band can justify a correlated gain vector as a cheap receive approximation.
