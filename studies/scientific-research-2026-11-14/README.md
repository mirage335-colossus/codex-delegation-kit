# Scientific research depth, automatic initiation and development uptake

This exploratory study used pumpModem's Astra/Fable-labelled research and
appraisals to refine the kit. The goal is sustained scientific grounding when
an ordinary development task needs it, without requiring the user to ask for
research or make research the main chat's only activity. A dedicated assignment
uses existing permitted expert roles and coordinator-dispatched discovery
helpers; no new registered role, model pin or runtime capability was added.

## What was observed

The final ordinary-development trial demonstrated the desired workflow in one
case. Both versions requested research. The revised-policy owner requested a
dedicated sustained lead plus two discovery branches, continued its native code
audit in parallel, and used the resulting synthesis in its engineering plan.
The baseline requested two bounded literature branches and a focused engineering
consultation. The substantive difference was depth and purpose of the assignment,
not a newly enabled binary ability to browse.

The dedicated lead's reusable [synthesis](results/a84-synthesis.md), together with
the [HF](results/a84-hf.md) and [EME](results/a84-eme.md) evidence, compares physical
models and receiver alternatives, tracks source access and conditions, and maps
them to present code. A directly inspected HF paper changed the proposed
relationship between group-delay motion and phase/Doppler motion. The owner also
specified a confidently wrong, nonempty Helper-narrowing control. The baseline
already proposed matched IQ and fixed-bank comparisons, so those are not credited
as uniquely caused by the new policy.

Read the [owner's reviewed amendment](results/a84-owner-reviewed.md) with the
[original handoff](results/a84-owner.md) and [source errata](evaluation/source-errata.md).
Independent review corrected author order, a year in the lead synthesis, and
clarified that separately reported marginal percentiles do not define a measured
joint event. The original reports remain intact. This is reviewed uptake, not
one-shot correctness. No modem implementation, receiver test, RF experiment or
hardware qualification was performed.

The trial was resource-nonconforming: the candidate EME scout used 32 individual
web operations against 14, making assistance 67 against the shared ceiling of 60
(52 initially requested). The baseline EME scout used 23 against 12. Original
logs, prefix attributions and coordinator interventions remain visible. Useful
research and the observed activation are distinct from budget compliance.
This study establishes neither cost savings nor reliable automatic behavior
across tasks, and does not equate the output with the entire historical corpus.

The initial treatment added explicit family comparison, source conditions,
equivalence and development consequences to the existing policy and the user's
research boilerplate. The first comparison showed mixed, complementary findings.
V2 added a task-wide coverage check rather than repeating evidence for the chosen
algorithm. After the user's clarification, v3 made autonomous sustained research
the explicit responsibility of the main coordinator/engineering owner and tested
it with an ordinary task containing no research request. A final source-review
correction strengthened the marginal-versus-joint evidence distinction.

The delivered policy activates this work for consequential scientific gaps,
reuses adequate existing curated research, preserves a substantial appendix
behind compact coordination, and lets independent development continue.
Scouts, engineering owners, workers and reviewers have matching responsibilities.
Controller prompts request missing evidence through the existing coordinator;
their no-network and supplied-context no-tools restrictions remain unchanged.
User-curated _ref and _appraisal require explicit curation authorization;
agent knowledge belongs in an authorized research location or task evidence.

## Evidence and reproduction

See [method and exemplar provenance](evaluation/method-and-exemplars.md),
[independent comparison](evaluation/independent-review.md),
[automatic activation review](evaluation/autonomous-review.md),
[routing observations](evaluation/autonomous-routing.md), and the
[frozen autonomous criteria](evaluation/autonomous-criteria.md).
The independent reviewer initially received opaque report IDs rather than an arm
map; wording could reveal treatment, so successful blinding is not established.

All original prompts are under inputs. Discovery, uptake, continuation and
autonomous freeze JSON files record hashes at their respective dispatch points;
initial-results-lock.json preserves the six initial reports. The requested native
role for matched owners/researchers was sol_expert, with luna_scout discovery
support where actually requested. The source changed during this chat, but the
session did not reload edited role instructions: comparisons use a common
requested role and baseline policy with appended treatment. Runtime-resolved
settings, complete native tool traces, token/allowance telemetry and matched
consumed-resource measurements were unavailable.

To repeat, use fresh context-limited assignments with the exact frozen common
inputs and treatment, original requested roles/permissions and equal ceilings.
Keep answer-bearing exemplars and other-arm artifacts inaccessible to
participants. For ordinary development, dispatch assistance only when the owner
actually requests it, preserve overlap messages, and evaluate both the produced
research and its owner uptake. Assess individual operations, failures, primary
support and corrections rather than tool-call batches or citation totals.
Native dispatch messages remain in the original chat; these files are not a
complete executable replay of its tool runtime.

Source audits need pumpModem at the recorded revision with cited files; this
study does not bundle that repository. Procedural isolation on a shared
filesystem is not an OS sandbox. The verifier's mistakenly placed temporary
study artifact was preserved in the kit and cleaned from pumpModem; no curated
files were modified by this task. Later unrelated local changes were preserved.

Final kit validation:

    python3 -B -m unittest discover -s tests -v

All 313 tests passed in 48.587 seconds; see [retained output](evaluation/kit-tests.txt).
Targeted backend and distribution checks also passed after automatic-initiation
wording was added. They establish delivered prompt content, unchanged role pins,
bundle compatibility and configuration preservation, not model compliance.
The installer inventory remains 23 files. Study artifacts are source-only
evaluation evidence, not additional runtime instructions.

## Completion and deployment disposition

The mandatory Astra completion review accepted the source changes and exercises.
A prepared global installer invocation was rejected by automatic approval before
process creation because explicit deployment approval was required. The user then
selected: "Keep the changes in the repository only." No deployment was retried,
and the active global kit was not updated. The reviewer subsequently returned
"ready to conclude" for the source-only outcome.

Changes remain uncommitted and unpushed in the source checkout at base
08fc5207af39b9dd728d83ffeaadd4d1b4815653. Unrelated user files were preserved.
The deployed kit therefore retains its earlier instructions; future installation
is outside this task's final authorized scope.
