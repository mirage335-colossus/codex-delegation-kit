# Frozen evaluation rubric (before participant dispatch)

This is an exploratory matched prompt study, not a benchmark of historical model versions. Compare each answer to the requested case and relevant combined Astra/Fable exemplar slice, not the entire multi-domain corpus. Historical reports are leads and evaluation targets, not unquestioned ground truth. Valid alternative coverage can satisfy a criterion; do not require reproducing a particular paper list or wording.

For each dimension record 0 (missing/misleading), 1 (partial), or 2 (substantive and supported), with concrete answer/source locators. Preserve disagreements and invalid claims. Scores are descriptive; no arbitrary aggregate pass threshold establishes task completion.

1. Decision-relevant coverage of distinct mechanisms and model families.
2. Primary-source identity, claim support and honest access/publication status.
3. Foundational and recent search coverage, including genuine remaining gaps.
4. Contrary evidence and reconciliation of regime/measurement definitions.
5. Equivalence, useful combinations, simpler alternatives and remaining capability gaps.
6. Implementation/audit implications and discriminating expected observations.
7. Calibrated limits: source support versus proposals, measured results and qualification.
8. Scope/resource discipline and useful, reusable synthesis without filler.

Discovery: independently verify consequential claims against primary sources. Do not retroactively credit a participant for sources or methods the verifier finds. Keep over-budget material visible but exclude it from eligible comparisons. Count useful distinctions and omissions, not citation totals. A single pair, requested model label and equal ceilings establish neither reliability, equal used budgets nor savings.

HF targets from combined exemplars: stationary controls versus time-varying conditions; shift/drift/spread and width definitions; Gaussian versus other correlation/spectral shapes with applicability; coherent multipath and cross-frequency dependence; severe presets versus an envelope; noise/outage/front-end limits; measured replay, stochastic and physical/hybrid alternatives; truth versus receiver knowledge; practical validation and false-admission/resource costs. These targets are not an exhaustive oracle.

EME held-out targets are evaluated against the reserved Astra/Fable EME chapters after the treatment freezes. No EME result may be used to revise the candidate before the held-out comparison. Report that the domain comes from the same project/corpus, rather than claiming independent cross-project generalization.

Uptake: separately evaluate observable source audit and recommendation changes. Does the participant connect a cited finding to actual existing implementation, avoid duplicate capability work without erasing the remaining gap, reconcile dated plans with code/receipts, preserve negative/conditional outcomes and actual qualification limits, and honor the user's artifact boundary? A written plan is not executed implementation. Actual code changes and product measurements are outside this task.
