# Uptake evidence evaluation key

Evaluate whether an answer uses the supplied evidence to revise a concrete
continuation or uptake decision. Credit equivalent well-supported reasoning;
do not require exact wording. A plan is not an executed code change, and a
documented check is not a check performed by the answerer.

## Scoring cues

- **Substantive:** source/API link plus capability boundary; accurate chronology;
  conditional positive and negative findings; correct no-prior-reallocation
  fallback; missing final-source receiver cohort; no false claim of executed
  validation; respects reserved directories.
- **Partial:** gets the broad uptake direction but misses one material distinction
  (for example, treats the staged shadow as receiver success, or notices the OU
  API but misses its calibration limits).
- **Misleading:** proposes a duplicate generic OU implementation without source
  audit; claims the research proposal remains unimplemented as current fact;
  discards the weak-case loss; promotes a selected shadow to universal receiver
  evidence; restores missing prior mass; says final receiver cohort passed under
  fallback; or proposes edits to the reserved source evidence.

No production implementation is requested or evaluated here.
