## Important disagreement and transfer limits

The Fable 40 “delay percentile / diffuse fraction” discussion reports its own integration of historical LL67 surface laws: surface-area versus projected-area weighting changes inferred cross-section and yields materially different t50 estimates. It contrasts those calculations with K1JT10's projected-area results. These are competing weighting/estimation choices, not two direct 5.8-GHz measurements. Keep the inputs and integral visible; do not silently promote either result to truth. Likewise Fable's 5.2-cm (near 5.8 GHz) values are interpolation/modeling from 3.8- and 23-cm measurements, not exact-frequency observation. Its 5.76-GHz width figures attributed to K1JT10 are model predictions; width definitions (spectral correlation width versus occupied-power bandwidth) must not be conflated.

A newer source gives a useful counterpoint without closing the empirical gap: Li et al., “Modeling and Characterization of Broadband Earth-Moon-Earth Communication Channels,” *Journal of Electronics & Information Technology* 48(6), 2026, DOI [10.11999/JEIT251028](https://jeit.ac.cn/en/article/doi/10.11999/JEIT251028). The official journal search result exposes the abstract (page returned 403 on open; abstract only, no full text inspected). It proposes quasi-specular plus diffuse statistical channel modeling, PDP and orbital Doppler, with numerical/Monte Carlo validation; reported examples are simulations at 1.296 GHz. This is model validation against its own computation, not measured-channel validation. Its reported delay/Doppler/BER conclusions must not be transferred to 5.8 GHz without matching assumptions and measurements.
