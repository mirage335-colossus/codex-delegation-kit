# Source errata for the ordinary development exercise

The raw research and owner handoffs retain their original wording. This
record gives the independent verification to use for the reviewed handoff.

## Northern HF measurement strata

The publisher lists the authors as **E. M. Warrington, A. J. Stocker, and
D. R. Siddle**. Its Table 3 places the 5.8 MHz Svalbard–Kiruna values
12.0 ms composite multipath, 1.5 ms effective multipath, and 2 Hz composite
Doppler in the 2003 column. It places the 20 MHz Kirkenes–Kiruna values
5.3 ms, 0.4 ms, and 39.2 Hz in the **2004** column. The lead's synthesis
misattributes the author order and gives 2005 for the latter condition. The
original owner handoff misattributes the author order and gives no year.

Table 3 gives a 95% occurrence summary for each separate width under the
stated peak-to-mean power-ratio filter (>3.5). These are grouped rows of
marginal statistics. The article supplies no evidence that the three
quoted widths occurred together in an individual observation or establish
a joint 95% severity envelope. Composite multipath width is detected
support, effective width uses the study's specific modal-power weighting,
and its composite Doppler width is the narrowest band containing 80% of
signal power after noise correction. The numbers may anchor explicitly
synthetic stress settings with documented assumptions. Use separate
conditions rather than combining maxima into an observed channel or
mapping them to an outage probability. The 2003 Svalbard sample is from July
only. The footnote reports azimuth-array sidelobes at 14.4 and 20 MHz on the
Kirkenes path; do not extend that stated caveat to the quoted delay and
Doppler columns without evidence.

[Warrington, Stocker and Siddle, Radio Science 41, RS2006 (2006), author record, Table 3 and §§3.1–3.3](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2005RS003294)
