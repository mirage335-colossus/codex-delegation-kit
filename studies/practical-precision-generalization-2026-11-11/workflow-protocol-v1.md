# Autonomous numerical review and owner repair

Frozen before workflow development and transfer. One-shot v5 is retained as
three numerical-behavior passes and one miss (j21), despite four product passes.
The final horizontal diagnostic qualified a change from implicit wider operands
to float and reduced median end-to-end time from 64.087 to 56.457 ms (7/9 paired
wins). j21 also omitted live arrays from a diagnostic byte report. No earlier
record is replaced or relabeled as autonomously repaired.

The user's constraint is no user steering. A single unreviewed model completion
was an experimental choice, not that constraint. This phase tests the existing
owner / independent read-only acceptance / owner repair architecture. It adds no
runtime registry, role, model pin or enforcement mechanism.

## Frozen packets and routing

Every actor receives exact `prompt-v6.txt` once as engineering-guidance.txt.
Owners receive the same ordinary product task and bounded implementation packet
as earlier, with their result explicitly proposed for independent acceptance.
Fresh independent reviewers receive `review-packet-v1.txt`, ordinary task/source,
public checks and owner evidence only. They receive no private tests, witness,
previous case solutions or root-supplied engineering findings.

Use a fresh Luna/high owner and a fresh `inherited_reviewer` with no model/effort
override. This preserves the primary model's requested inheritance and existing
read-only review role; effective runtime settings remain unobserved. The original
owner makes repairs from the reviewer's actual findings. The reviewer then checks
the affected source and contracts. Reviewers may write diagnostic scratch only,
and target-source hashes verify that they did not change the implementation.

Coordinator actions are mechanical: deliver frozen packets, relay the reviewer's
complete findings unchanged, preserve snapshots, enforce stage/aggregate budgets,
and run the frozen evaluator. Do not add engineering hints, select fixes, repair
participant source or disclose private results before workflow completion.
The numerical independent assessment after the experiment may diagnose failures;
it does not count as part of an unsteered accepted workflow.

## Budget and stages

One workflow has at most 90 tool invocations and 45 minutes from owner dispatch:
30 tools / 15 minutes for initial implementation, 20 tools / 10 minutes for the
first review, and at most two repair rounds of 10 tools / 5 minutes for the owner
plus 10 tools / 5 minutes for re-review. Unused allowance does not reset a stage
or erase failures. Ordinary tool feedback and autonomous repairs are allowed.
Exhaustion, missing acceptance or required user tuning is a workflow failure.

Before any repair, archive the exact proposed source, public evidence and review
findings. Archive each repair and acceptance stage separately. Mechanical product
grading occurs after the whole workflow, and may also inspect retained drafts
without disclosing results to participants. A review acceptance is not a claim
that every possible optimization or every untested input has been proven.

## Development and prospective transfer

Develop this workflow on a new copy of retired j21. Label it workflow development,
with a separately declared budget; it does not restart or repair the historical
one-shot run. A fresh reviewer must discover relevant problems without receiving
the known horizontal declarations, omitted arrays or timing result. If needed,
refine the generic packet before freezing transfer; retain all development results.

Then run two complete workflows on each of two independently authored, genuinely
new families: nonlinear curve processing with replacement-algorithm/narrow-bulk
opportunity, and rational timeline resampling with exact/local-wide state plus
substantial narrow work. Preflight product requirements, viable witnesses and
meaningful bad controls before dispatch. Read scopes are instructions rather than
OS-enforced blinding.

Acceptance keeps the three established decisions: sufficient arithmetic,
necessary work, qualified useful default. Also verify evidence fidelity and
resource reporting; no hidden speed ratio or particular algorithm is added.
Report initial proposals, independent findings, owner repairs and final accepted
workflow separately. The final result is autonomous multi-agent development,
not a claim of flawless one-shot prompting. After four prospective workflows,
update source/reporting and request current mandatory completion review.
