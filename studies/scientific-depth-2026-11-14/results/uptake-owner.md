# pumpModem channel and receiver development plan

## Decision and order

Start with **calibrated capture and replay, then fix the bottlenecks the fixtures actually separate**. Preserve one native `IndependentApskV2` emitter and one `CompleteStreamReceiver` with its configured UTC/keyed calendar. A new environmental layer should describe the source, propagation and receive planes and produce auditable replay or a bounded native simulation configuration. It must never select public hypotheses from the realized channel. Do not begin with a new equalizer, lunar waveform engine, full-wave ground solver or another stream receiver.

Record gain/phase/noise/validity at explicit planes and keep evaluator truth separate. Freeze exact build/source, immutable IQ/mask and per-record public profile. | Sign, absolute gain/noise, rate/time, phase and full-aperture/tail controls close on known input; scalar and disabled controls retain baseline behavior. |

Retain the declared residual prior. An operational change needs better or acceptably equivalent communication at matched false-event risk and a complete-service cost result. |

Steps 1a and 1b can proceed independently of later propagation fitting. Step 2 planning products can be used offline before receiver changes. Thresholds in the supplied fixtures (VLF 3 dB at seven points, EME ±100 Hz candidate) are diagnostics, not deployment acceptance requirements. The exercise explicitly retains ±300 Hz EME public center support. Pump requirements are canonical C++, public hypotheses/calendar and physical quiet completion, memory/worker limits and demanding **complete receiver** throughput; there is no universal error in dB, FP64 agreement, field failure probability, or throughput-win percentage specified for these new proposals.

## What the constructed observations change

I ran [an independent CSV analysis](uptake-checks/transfer_checks.py) using Python 3.13.5, with [retained stdout](uptake-checks/transfer_checks.txt). It reads the 28 VLF and 21 EME rows, performs complex transfer division and conditional RLC reconstruction; independently computes the causal FIR's complex response and signed alias map, verifies them against every EME row, and evaluates one wider counterfactual. Each row is a separate single-tone calculation; there is no C/N₀, ADC noise, modulated signal, Helper, jitter, propagation fade, candidate scoring, timing observation or measured station in these inputs. No use of the exact constructed residual is proposed as a receiver feature.

### VLF: identical installed response, different repair

At each offset use the stated shared calibration: `H_up=H_end/H_mid→out`, where `H_mid→out` is observed with independent matched midpoint injection. Compare complex ratios within each fixture; multiplying two series responses commutes and the initial end-to-end curves alone do not localize the bottleneck. Negative/positive probe edges below are −112.5/+112.5 Hz around 8 kHz; phase is relative to the supplied center phase.

The series model `H=1/[1+jQ(f/f0−f0/f)]` fits *these* two isolated responses. At this center the model predicts ~39.47 Hz symmetric cascade span for either S0 and for V02/S1, and ~254.85 Hz for V01/S1, at the −3 dB crossing. These are interpolated model estimates, not more fixture observations or a profile guarantee. For a V02-like upstream resonator, a narrower slower public profile is a viable controlled experiment, with absolute center gain, spectral skirt, pulse duration, drift, latency, Helper placement/power, calendar and quiet cost repriced from the actual prepared waveform. A fixture with an unavailable intermediate calibration or very low downstream gain cannot reliably use division (loading, isolation and error amplification); use source currents, termination/reference changes or a separate transfer standard. An inverse at −16.4 dB raises downstream noise amplitudes ~6.6× at that frequency; it can calibrate deterministic distortion but cannot create lost energy or recover clipping. Q broadening can reduce peak current/launched power or admit blockers; repeat two-level and load/temperature sweeps, test phase and actual DATA plus Helper and guards across and between probes, then compare valid physical completion on withheld full frames. Distinguish profile experiments (different transmitted waveforms through matched measured conditions) from receiver scoring comparisons (same input and waveform).

### EME: correct center support, then preserve the observations it refers to

With positive frequency `exp(+j 2π f t)`, multiply by `exp(−j 2π∫ f_predict dt)`; for each probe, `f_post = f_pre − f_mix` and received center `c = f_post − probe_offset`. The wider witness is the reference both for signed frequency and relative complex gain. The 2,400→600 candidate applies `H(f_post)` **at the incoming signed frequency** and then folds into `[−300,300)`; an output frequency alone is not a pre-mix or pre-decimation measurement.

The present candidate FIR has a 50-input-sample group lag (20.833 ms, 12.5 samples at its output) and 100-input-sample finite memory (41.667 ms to drain after a last impulse), distinct from lunar arrival and scatter. Compensate time labels only once; retain prehistory/invalid halos, implemented decimation phase and tails even when timestamps are compensated. The complex full nominal support is ±(300+112.5)=±412.5 Hz, so centered complex sampling needs **strictly >825 samples/s with margin**, before pulse skirts, residual scatter definitions, Helper, transition band, oscillator and hardware uncertainty. The actual coarse planner in `src/triad_receiver_coarse.cpp:261–311` also requires protected half-width strictly below `.4 Fs` (nominal DATA-only implies `Fs>1031.25`, hence <67.5 Hz extra if `Fs=1200`); it chooses an integer divisor with the allowed edge. `src/robust_profile.cpp:286–321` separately guards real audio plus Helper, declared frequency motion and skirts against DC/Nyquist. `PublicProfile` requires valid positive nominal DATA IF; the signed complex fixture's zero-centered axes are not a drop-in 1.2 ksample/s real-audio profile.

As a desk candidate only, for the already idealized 2,400 complex samples/s post-mix input, decimate by two with the same-length FIR at nominal cutoff 500 Hz. In the provided tones the worst deviations were −0.0193/+0.0021 dB for L03; none folds. A 1/16 Hz grid gave −0.0206/+0.0061 dB over the nominal DATA support; ±787.5…±1200 Hz can fold *onto* that support in one decimation and the grid maximum there was −67.595 dB (not a continuous or blocker-specific guarantee). Start engineering with retained wide IQ/adequately validated full public support; use this filter as a falsifiable DATA-only reference, not the chosen deployment rate. Define required ripple/alias rejection from measured blockers and noise/ADC headroom and verify worst transitions, sample-rate conversion, Helper/quiet support and complete work. For low-rate optimization use overlapping derotated subbands only after wide acquisition, common coordinates/timestamps, retained prior mass and an explicit union coverage certificate; share preparation and never infer quiet from holes, a single narrow band, or a confidence failure. A wider digital tap after an inadequate analog/RF/mixer/ADC gate cannot diagnose what is already lost; preserve a pre-reduction witness, independently capture out-of-band blockers and use safe analog tuning or frequency translation first.

Discriminator: create one immutable **wide** calibrated capture for each contained, asymmetric-edge and displaced/folded case, both DATA bits and blank/line/blocked controls, plus noisy and time-varying holdouts. Generate four deterministic views (current versus sufficient anti-alias observation; full center prior vs ±100 proposed center coverage). Within each observation keep IQ/mask/parameters identical and run search coverage as an explicit diagnostic restriction with original ±300 prior weights, no operational renormalization. Between observation views derive both from the same wide input and account for their actual filter/ENBW/delay/validity; they are deliberately different observations, so not a likelihood-only A/B. Check signed residual and gain versus witness at each plane, alias markers, mask/tail and complete slot outcomes. Repeat near both signs of the public bound, phase/timing discontinuities, filter edges and clock/LO errors separately. Bounds from these traces cannot estimate acquisition, BER, relative scatter, occurrence or a field tracking margin. Narrower *public* support needs independent station/radio evidence across relevant epochs and a deliberate public-profile decision.

## Implementation contract for the first increment

Create a versioned **offline** appraisal record with `measured | predicted | synthetic` provenance, model/revision/validity domain, uncertainty and missing reason; document RF/IF/baseband plane, amplitude vs power reference and units, accepted/emitted/delivered/available power (no implicit EMRP/ERP/EIRP conversion), UTC and sample/LO clock basis, signed center vs PSD 2σ vs defined power quantile vs hard support, polarization basis and observation aperture. Store antenna/feed/site state, analog and digital transfer/latency, PSD, masks and sharp/nonlinear limits. Put station-declared `ReceiverAssumptions` and replay metadata in a separate object forwarded to the public profile. A separate evaluator-only sidecar retains injected bits, underlying paths, events, seeds and expected source/tail data. Use truth only to evaluate support/misses after receiver outputs are committed. Do not use planning outputs, generated reality, receiver decisions or known episode length to set trial noise variance, search priors, aperture or physical completion. Replay must use lossless exact sample and validity encoding, first sample/tick, rate/clock/IF, SHA-256 plus schema/units, stable chunking or a declared chunk-variation control, and bounded queues/receipts.

For narrow linear channels, use a local one-port complex transfer with optional path rows: `H(t,f)=Σ a_k(t,f)·[u_rᴴJ_k(t,f)u_t]·exp(jφ_k(t)−j2π f τ_g,k(t))`. Use a transfer tensor/two-port for local conducted/near E/H cases rather than this plane-wave polarization projection. For real nondispersive delay `τ=L/c`, group delay and carrier phase come from the same retarded path: `φ=−2πf_cτ`, `Δf=−f_c dτ/dt` in this sign convention. In dispersive ionospheric/modal paths keep group delay and reference carrier phase separately. Apply propagation once; a moving complex delay/phase operator must not then receive the same Doppler again. Near a notch a phase slope need not be causal propagation lag (the shared 0/7 ms near-equal two-tap case yields apparent ~−693 or +707 ms as the ratio crosses one). Keep physical finite support and validity rather than searching a spurious huge arrival window. Use power sums for resolved/incoherent statistics only; combine components as voltages for the rendered sample. Report delay support, centered spread, early-power fraction and estimator resolution/censoring separately.

Map scalar, static frequency/delay, fixed/bounded two-ray and selected existing gain laws into `StreamChannelConfig` in its native simulation setup; new physical profiles are opt-in and leave `Off` exact. For a verified complex response that cannot map to those controls, add bounded causal LTI coefficients or a small set of differential paths at `StreamingChannel::Impl` where composite TX history is interpolated/combined, ahead of RX noise. Tag whether phase is split or mapped and distinguish current legacy fixed-echo RF phase arithmetic from moving RF+IF arithmetic. If fitting a broadband hardware response, model appropriate injection/noise planes around it; do not blindly put all calibrated effects on the propagation multiplier. Put actual site geometry calculations, P.368/P.533 queries, mode solving and lunar integration outside the sample hot path. Use one source-history ring with max *excess* delay, finite interpolation/filter halos, exact coordinate/reference mapping for bulk arrival, deterministic independent seeds and a checked per-scenario memory/CPU cost. Reject undefined rates/references or out-of-domain “physical” inputs, keep explicit synthetic controls and report unsupported scope. For multipath and noise failure mechanisms carry separate observable states: fade/physical no-arrival with present noise, coherent cancellation, unusable dynamic range or coverage, invalid samples/timebase, unresolved path, and conservative receiver erasure/ambiguity. Only valid adequately observed quiet can advance completion.

## Environmental scenario cards and actionable tests

Keep these profiles conditional on the actual path, endpoint hardware and observation. None is a set of independent population percentiles to combine into “worst.” A severe synthetic combination is useful if its components and causal timing are declared, including a separate matched negative control; assign no field probability to it. Preserve script/source/standard identity and which claims came from primary figures, modeled assumptions or derived arithmetic. The frozen [handoff](../inputs/uptake-handoff.md) points to source-access ledgers and reproducible research checks. This plan uses those records and independent calculations on the new probes; it does not repeat external discovery when no source would resolve missing station inputs.

**Sub-9 kHz: choose geometry and launch before DSP.** For a local loop, record applied voltage/current, turns/loop area, effective inductance/loss, receiving-axis alignment and complex loading. In a quasistatic magnetic geometry use `m=NIA`, (B) scaling as (m/r³), and receive EMF proportional to frequency times loop area and the normal (B); test the orientation null, source separation and a second current/termination to discriminate near field, conduction/return path and leakage. For electrodes/earth-current, prefer a site-conditioned impedance/transfer two-port (electrode/soil/water geometry, seasonal conductivity and contact/load variation), not free-space gain or generic (E/H=377Ω). For a remotely launched field use current/effective moment, efficiency and directional launch reference, e.g. a short top-loaded vertical with losses/matching/voltage limits, or a loop under its intended capture geometry. Measure actual field using calibrated electric and/or magnetic axes and convert once to the receive port using antenna factor/calibration. Coil/ferrite loss, saturation, detuning and electrode contact all affect feasible settings. Treat mechanical/magnetoelectric or direct antenna modulation and narrow-line operational successes as alternatives only after comparable delivered power, spectrum, gate/retune, lifetime and actual modulation have been shown; a sub-Hz coherent success is evidence of narrowband sensitivity, not 225 Hz flatness.
