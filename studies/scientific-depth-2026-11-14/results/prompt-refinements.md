# Prompt refinement and evidence boundaries

The source change makes substantial scientific research an explicit responsibility when gaps in the mechanism, alternatives, empirical assumptions or operating envelope could change ordinary development. It retains a dedicated scientific lead and independent discovery branches within existing roles; it adds no registered role, model permission or runtime scheduler.

The prospective candidate supplied to the initial engineering owner is preserved byte-for-byte in [candidate-policy.md](../inputs/candidate-policy.md), with its hash in [the initial freeze](../prospective-freeze.json). That is the input associated with the observed [self-initiation](activation.md). Subsequent refinement must not be described as having retroactively caused that first response.

The broader exercise sharpened these instructions:

- Define coverage across the entire requested operating envelope and require explanatory, empirical and quantitative depth in every material domain. A good chapter or bibliography cannot compensate for an absent branch.
- Give research a sustained objective and useful follow-up allocations. Preserve detailed reusable findings behind a compact engineering handoff.
- Perform available scientific calculations, reference comparisons and public-observation checks when they determine a design choice. Keep their scope separate from product execution and qualification.
- Independently find omissions and incorrect interpretations, return feasible gaps for correction, and retain original attempts and changed judgments.
- Retrieve and apply findings at concrete development boundaries; carry source conditions into the choice, affected interface and discriminating check. Reopen the choice when new observations conflict.
- Keep user-curated `_ref/` and `_appraisal/` read-only absent an explicit request to curate them. Resolve agent research output paths against the authorized repository.

During the exercise, primary-source checks exposed confusion between predictions and observations, and discrepancies between source numbers and reconstructed values. The final source policy consequently adds an explicit instruction to inspect results and limitations, carry the measured comparison and conventions into the decision, and retain/reconcile reconstruction differences. This is an adaptive refinement informed by this exercise, not a separately randomized or independently replicated prompting result.

The corresponding compact cues are wired through the existing scout, worker and expert/reviewer role instructions and backend-delivered prompts. Supplied-context and no-network profiles retain their authority limits. The 23-file installation inventory and pinned models/efforts remain unchanged. [Source validation](../evaluation/source-checks.md) reports the full 313-test pass and the six meter tests, including earlier failures and their correction. These tests verify wiring and compatibility, not scientific quality.

Everything remains in this Git checkout. The user declined global installation; no deployment, publishing, model-setting change or pumpModem implementation is part of this result. The scientific exercise itself is a coordinated demonstration under finite allocations and corrective review, not evidence that prompt text enforces compliance or guarantees automatic breadth in every future task.
