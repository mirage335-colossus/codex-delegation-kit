### Noise and receiver choice across bands

Select the simplest useful noise representation by site and stage. Begin with input-referred thermal plus actual antenna/sky contribution and measured linear transfer/ENBW. Add physically conditioned background and deterministic lines or bursts at the appropriate plane, with amplitude probability, PSD and scatter/cross-feed correlations from measurement. VLF lightning/mains and HF radio emissions can be dominant even when ADC noise is small. EME T_sys, pointing, preselector/RF compression, LO phase noise with blockers, passband occupancy and receiver gain can dominate after geometry. For disruptive front-end conditions, measure fixed-gain blocker sweeps, noisy terminations, AGC states/recovery, ENOB scale, intermod and reciprocal mixing before labeling a DSP improvement. In simulation, a passed ADC clipping flag is a model diagnostic, and available real instrument flags are independent input quality evidence. Compatibility with phase/noise claims must be shown at actual stages and configured rate. [Cross-band noise and instrument measurements](../research/noise/chapter.md), [ITU-R P.372 records and recommendation access limits](../research/lead/source-ledger.md)

Use three consecutive diagnostics before proposing a new estimator:

1. **Proposal failure versus signal loss.** On the same observation, compare full public retained/fallback proposal support against ordinary Helper-assisted ranking, including missing and wrong confidently assisted cases with the same Helper projection/noise and public weighting. Trace where the correct region drops out through private evaluator evidence, never decoder information. Measure whether restoring ranking or correcting RF/timing support recovers complete decoding before adding a model. Do not assume the common Helper phase is the actual DATA gain for separated frequencies/propagation modes.
2. **Shared trajectory versus delay/tap covariance.** On the same retained public coordinates, compare existing affine, coherent/AR gain, frequency mixtures and drift with a matched calibrated delayed basis. Both bit templates and the normalized null must survive any shared sufficient-statistic representation. Use an unweighted scalar, small known delay/low-rank basis, independent gains and physically different covariance as separate controls. Matched off-delay tails, branch cost and field holdouts can separate a genuine receiver improvement from a simulator-specific fit.
3. **Null mismatch versus DATA mismatch.** Compare preselection or measured whitening to the existing single-line nuisance, with matched energy, filter-support and false-admission rule. A small bounded fixed covariance filter trained on independent noise and applied to observations and both hypotheses is the first conditional candidate for nonwhite but linear receiver inputs. Clip/blanking/saturation is nonlinear and changes both hypotheses and the quiet aperture; a quality-aware/erasure-aware receiver is later work with complete native and null evidence. Bounded supplemental evidence can be evaluated on the existing internal scorer only after workspace sizing and observing supported benefits. [Receiver comparison and counterexamples](../research/receiver/chapter.md), [native integration and sufficiency limits](../research/native/chapter.md), [measurement and validation evidence](../research/validation/chapter.md)


## Native implementation and validation contracts

**Appraisal boundary.** Extend profile preparation near `src/robust_profile.cpp` with an offline or low-rate native evaluator that can be used by a probe and the GUI. Use typed fields or units in names and a versioned evidence declaration. Inputs are HF/VLF/microwave RF frequency, path and mode, endpoint gains/coupling, reference planes, time/geometry, noise and front-end characterization, a bounded weather/event input where relevant, and the validity/support region for each model. Output should include absolute and conditional received levels, N0 with its reference plane and bandwidth, predicted C/N0 ranges, public bulk arrival and CFO support, mode delay/Doppler support, separate front-end passband/headroom limits, and reasons for absent paths. Keep scenario measurements/uncertainty distinct from the resolved controls. Changing target planning C/N0 alone never changes synthesized noise or passband. Any prediction of no usable path is a model result/operational planning statement, not a receiver mute command. Export the detailed declaration, not hundreds of post-hoc scalar estimates.

**Simulation boundary.** Keep `EmittedSource` and `StreamingChannel::read` unchanged as data path interfaces. Map each versioned physical condition to a bounded native set of excess delays, complex gains, center Dopplers or smooth phase/range tracks plus noise and frontend parameters. Linear time-varying baseband can be expressed as `y(t)=Σ a_i(t) exp(j φ_i(t)) s(t-τ_0(t)-δτ_i(t)) + n(t)`. A moving geometric delay must apply consistently both to mapped time and carrier phase `−2πf_c τ(t)`; independent local oscillator or stochastic scatter terms keep separate seed domains. Never add the same geometric Doppler as both phase derivative and another oscillation. Use a bounded causal ring and existing fractional interpolation, per-contributor hop/calendar treatment, chronological gains, overflow-safe total/tail calculations, and declared capture/bandwidth bounds. For a single unresolved path, keep the cheaper scalar branch and existing Off path. For a fitted delay spectrum, use a bounded reduced quadrature/list and assess convergence against a larger desktop teacher under paired physical realization where available. Avoid post-fade block normalization or per-slot seed resets. A multi-channel polarization teacher may project to one real receiver feed at a declared hardware boundary. Coherent multi-feed native processing is a separate hardware capability decision, with a same-stream resource budget and measured calibration.

**Front-end boundary.** Use one explicit reference plane for conversion between dBm and squared normalized amplitude. For input temperature and losses, refer antenna, feed and receiver to a common plane; `N0=k T_sys` is total equivalent noise at that plane. Preserve additive source noise, external colored/noisy input and internal stage noise as physically located contributions. First-stage linear response and nonlinear headroom must precede subsequent filtering. Calibrated deep out-of-band stress needs wider processing or a bounded measured stage-level surrogate. Downstream in-band complex or PCM samples alone cannot reveal lost pre-stage energy deterministically. Model known retune/capture losses with validity or existing support semantics, fit observable quiet PSD in an independent interval, and record filter gain/group delay, clipping, level-dependent recovery and whether null assumptions are qualified. Use exact bypass for old configs. Distinguish fitted device transfer, synthetic curve, and observed telemetry. Preserve true sample timestamps; do not replace continuous time with callback time or use resampling to conceal gaps.

**Receiver boundary.** Prefer established hypotheses and prepared calibration before extending the scorer. Public deterministic compensation uses a declared UTC origin, timebase and residual support. Begin with common carrier and public arrival control for EME. Where predicted envelope shear itself affects the supported waveform, an implementation may map observations once or map public templates using the same native coordinate and cancellation machinery. Verify constant-delay/constant-CFO limits and correction sign, absolute vs excess delay, shared sample-clock mapping, round-trip vs one-way geometry, split physical oscillator vs projected Doppler, and input collection before decimation. Track Helper and DATA corrections consistently. Applying the exact private scenario motion is a diagnostic ceiling only. Keep all residual public hypotheses and branch priors; avoid a new dense Doppler/delay/polarization dimension in the full coarse scan. Observe whether long-trajectory known compensation makes the existing drift lane sufficient before implementing a new tracker. Where a residual scientifically supports a new DATA likelihood, use a conditional D/N distribution with signal and templates transformed consistently, wire bounded base/overlap workspace through `CompleteStreamReceiver`, carry model/version/reason receipts, and preserve original prior mass and unmodified quiet evidence. The existing supplement remains a viable isolated prototype for a whitened no-line conditional model. A source change to make it operational must preserve its paired sizing, both canonical branches, public hypothesis accounting and line-conditioned fallback. An unadmitted branch must be visible. For colored noise, an AR-type or short fixed whitening filter on samples and templates may be much simpler than a new DATA covariance. Estimate from independent data or propagate the estimation uncertainty. Strengthen null validation under mixture, filtering and parameter screening before use; onset, fades, gaps and failed branches may not produce invented quiet.

### Bounded pilot shape and matching rules

- Run deterministic component examples for identity, equivalent one ray, one fractional delay, one known two-ray notch/tail, one sign/clock/UTC boundary and one front-end headroom alignment. Replay at the supported chunk extremes and an irregular sequence. Preserve both a no-noise response reference and a paired known-level noisy control. Add the mask/full-fade differential even if nothing changes in the controller.
- For the first physically enriched simulation pilot, choose one benign, one adverse-with-signal and one outage/unavailable case for each eligible domain shift. Groundwave needs separate cases at the two frequencies and at least one coast/terrain discriminator. HF needs separate quiet, changing, high-latitude order and disturbed/outage exposures. EME needs single narrow central return, distributed delay/Doppler/polarization and lost pointing/site/receiver cases. Each case records which mechanisms are held fixed. Use the same emitted signal, message, power definitions, public forecast uncertainty and fixed observation aperture in paired comparisons.
- For receiver screening, begin with two independent public seeds or accepted field episodes and one reserved holdout per selected scenario, then concentrate additional trials where the baseline fails and the candidate mechanism could help. This is a proposed minimum engineering screen, not a sample size for reception probability. Each A/B trial sees the same timestamped I/Q, bits/calendars, reporting and score policies. In a cross-model comparison, pair underlying experiments and verify actual time support, reference power and masks. Compare results at both constant conducted TX power and comparable measured RF-plane C/N0 if power changes as part of the scenario. Matched software run identity and a shared seed do not establish matched waveform/analog exposure.
- Predefine the deployment-relevant margin from measured conditions and stakeholder service observations. For known-narrow robust cases, stop at an equivalent mechanism or insufficient gain. For weak/saturated cases, stop promoting a candidate if any apparent gain collapses on held-out episodes, increases false DATA or premature completion under the applicable null, or makes demanding same-stream complete service queue grow persistently. A pilot can reject a mechanism without supporting a positive field-rate claim. Set follow-up counts and one-sided interval/success targets after defining independent episode units and an actual deployment target.
- Compare apples-to-apples on declared oscillator quality, effective hardware affinity/quota, build options and precision fallback, and charge preprocessing, calibration material/state and concurrent owners. Show both the chosen sentinel cases and the required configured rows with applicable evidence. Full campaigns, unknown-address acquisition, hardware procurement and installing software are outside this assignment.

Use present automatic arithmetic classes for broad proposals and hold wider calibrated whitening, a larger delay covariance or dual-feed matching for the smallest demonstrated viable candidate group. A front-end quality flag must preserve raw I/Q and provenance unless the hardware stream genuinely lacked the data or a qualified policy proves that its useful aperture is unobservable. Instrumented flags are not automatically permission to discount sample modes from H0 or quietly harvest tail slots.

### Matched counterexamples to keep

- **Desired signal and front-end saturation.** Keep conducted desired power fixed, sweep an out-of-band blocker before versus after the first potentially nonlinear stage, and record in-band desired gain, false components, measured clip counts/recovery and quiet evidence. A narrower offline FFT bandwidth can hide a clipped blocker. At the same upstream C/N0 it need not restore useful informational gain. Include a benign attenuator control and a blocker-only null.
- **Gain normalization and simulator-dependent improvement.** Compare AWGN only, path variation only, and both at matched conducted power and equal independently measured input PSD. Repeat with deliberately equal received aperture power measured outside the operational decision, while preserving the actual amplitude time series. A flat RMS equalizer that is trained on the same DATA window can increase a simulated result by leaking ideal symbol periods, output power or underestimating heterogeneous noise.
- **Path-model advantage and teacher cross-family stability.** Compare existing exact affine/taps, varying and drift UI controls on identical I/Q. Test the selected model's own family plus physically plausible alternate covariance, mode births/deaths and device impairments. A teacher is allowed larger finite work/physical truth for diagnosis, can offer mismatch and observed-error ceiling, and cannot authorize a live raw-IQ branch on its own.
- **Outage and definite completion.** Pair identical receiver input through candidate end times where one teacher truly stopped emitting and another continued into complete fade at the receiver. Any receiver distinction on the paired prefix is a truth leak, unless it used an explicitly justified independently available setting/measurement different between pairs. Then replay exposed signal after the two slots and measure premature completion and residual erasures. Distinguish a physical end criterion based on observations from observation-independent transmitter completion. Repeat with attested capture holes that must preclude quiet.
- **Phase compensation and slot addressing.** Compare identical capture with error-free teacher compensation (ceiling), publicly available ephemeris with held-out residual and baseline uncorrected search. Match TX/RX timestamp origins and correction delay, CFO and slopes, while preserving epoch and ordinal. Inject wrong epoch/ordinal, wrong site/clock reference, and matched weak analog gain. A broad/unguarded search increase cannot explain a supposed physics advantage, and corrected samples do not prove identifiability of blind keys or addresses.
