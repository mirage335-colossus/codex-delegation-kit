# Scientific-depth and development-uptake exercise

The completed exercise demonstrates comparable decision-relevant scientific breadth/depth across all eight frozen domains and successful uptake by a fresh engineering owner, after substantial correction passes. See [the result and limitations](results/demonstration.md), [independent scientific adjudication](evaluation/final-adjudication.md), and [independent uptake review](evaluation/uptake-review.md). The initial ordinary development task triggered a sustained research assignment without asking for literature research.

This source-checkout exercise follows the narrower [research and uptake study](../scientific-research-2026-11-14/README.md). The earlier study demonstrated useful initiation and selected decisions but did not establish depth comparable to the full pumpModem reference collection. This exercise tests that larger objective explicitly.

The read-only comparator comprises 36 reference Markdown files, approximately 421,034 words before deduplication, plus appraisal examples of evidence and subsequent decisions. [Corpus inventory](evaluation/corpus-inventory.json) records source identity and hashes. Eight evaluator maps cover the combined corpus; [the frozen matrix](evaluation/coverage-matrix.json) consolidates 89 distinct criteria, 85 essential and four conditional. Word counts describe the comparator, not the success condition. [Acceptance criteria](evaluation/acceptance.md) require substantive scientific evidence and appropriate performed desk checks throughout every material domain, followed by demonstrated development uptake.

## Prospective design

The [ordinary development task](inputs/task.md) requests an implementation-ready appraisal across sub-9 kHz links, 1 and 30 MHz groundwave, HF skywave and passive 5.8 GHz EME. It does not request literature research. The fresh owner receives that task, a neutral environment and the candidate policy subsection. [Frozen input hashes](prospective-freeze.json) precede gathering. The [activation record](results/activation.md) preserves its subsequent request for a sustained scientific lead and independent branches.

The coordinator then supplies finite useful allocations, nine discovery branches and a synthesis lead. Detailed research lives in `research/` beneath this study. The owner continues code audit and desk calculations while that work proceeds. Researchers cannot procedurally read pumpModem `_ref/`, `_appraisal/`, evaluator answers or earlier study results. Reviewers independently compare scientific evidence against the frozen matrix and return material gaps for correction. Original attempts and changed dispositions must be retained. Final science is frozen before a fresh owner receives it for a subsequent development decision exercise.

The procedural access boundary is not a sandbox guarantee. One initial antenna participant accidentally exposed evaluator lines, reported the violation and was excluded before gathering. A fresh participant replaced it without inherited context; [the incident record](evaluation/boundary-incident.md) preserves the original and replacement identities and limits of attribution.

## Evidence and scope

The [request meter](tools/research_meter.py) and [recipe](inputs/web-recipe.md) count individual search/open/click/find/screenshot items, including batched requests and failed calls, against [prospective allocations](allocations.json). Receipts and `meter.json` account for wrapped calls only. They neither prevent unwrapped calls nor authenticate participants or prove scientific understanding. A returned tool result can itself report an access error. [Allocation history](evaluation/allocation-history.json) preserves prospective changes. Six focused tests include conservative charging if receipt creation fails; a real one-operation official-source request exercised the wrapper before research.

Small reproducible scientific calculations and appropriate public numerical-product checks are authorized. PumpModem edits, builds, receiver experiments, physical deployment, global installation, publishing and model-setting changes are outside scope. The curated pump directories remain untouched by this exercise. Scientific desk evidence must not be presented as production qualification or hardware measurement.

The three outcomes are adjudicated separately: observed self-initiation in this case, comparison of scientific breadth/depth across every domain, and actual use of the new findings by a fresh engineering owner. This is one guided, coordinated demonstration, not an estimate of automatic activation frequency, superiority over other prompts, universal corpus parity, or token-cost savings.
