## Calculation artifact

`hf_calcs.py` is a standard-library script with no external downloads or data. `hf_calcs.txt` is its actual stdout. It calculates exact analytical Gaussian width/correlation conversions under the ideal model equations in the chapter, and free-space delay-to-excess-path conversion `c·Δτ`. It validates internal arithmetic only; it is not a simulation or product check. The script uses `B=2σ`, Gaussian PSD with integral-normalized shape, FWHM `2√(2ln2)σ`, central 80% width `2·Φ⁻¹(0.9)σ`, and `R(Δt)=exp(−2π²σ²Δt²)`.

## Access gaps and contradictions preserved

- Full Watterson original paper content was not accessible via publisher in the returned search results; available primary abstract/bibliographic records plus full ITU F.1487 suffice for model structure and normative width convention, but not its detailed raw measurement statistics.
- Full numerical tables in Basler et al. 1988 were not exposed; only the primary abstract was usable, so the reported “hundreds of microseconds / tens of hertz” stays explicitly abstract-level.
- P.531-16 was identified as current official ITU source but not opened beyond official current page/contents; it is used only to keep trans-ionospheric effects separate from HF skywave.
- High-latitude paper observation bins offer conditional P95s with mismatched measurement/threshold definitions. They do not establish a joint extreme delay-Doppler occurrence. F.1487 occurrence statement also lists marginal thresholds. The controlled stress product and the observed conditional path data must stay separate.
- Project source code's chosen `HF Worst` knob combination is a deliberate stress profile. Agreement with some F.1487 numerical values does not prove equivalence to its Gaussian two-tap model or establish that nature produces the tuple at a known frequency.
