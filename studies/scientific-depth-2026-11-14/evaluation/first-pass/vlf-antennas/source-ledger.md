# VLF antenna source ledger

Search/publication context: current task run `vlf-antennas`, metered allowance 80 logical web operations. Searches used combinations of: small low-frequency antenna and radiation resistance; VLF receiver loop/E-field/noise; Chu/Bode-Fano; 8.4 kHz ground antenna; earth-electrode mode; mechanical/magnetoelectric antennas; receiver dynamic range and ambient noise. Search results were leads only; primary passages below were inspected where available. Searches/open attempts consumed by the supplied meter; recent alternatives were retained even where evidence was limited. The branch does not claim a systematic or exhaustive review.

| [Reuveni et al., “Natural atmospheric noise statistics from VLF measurements in the eastern Mediterranean,” Radio Science (2010)](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2009RS004336) | Direct Wiley full text, §§2–4 inspected; 386-day Negev record, two orthogonal 81m² loops, 75 Hz bins at 4/7.5/10/26 kHz.
