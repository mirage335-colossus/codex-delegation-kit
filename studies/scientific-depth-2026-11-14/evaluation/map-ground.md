## How to use this map

Treat the 16 criteria as material parity dimensions, not a checklist of mandatory citations or exact figures. A candidate passes a dimension when it explains the same physical distinctions, gives enough evidence/definitions to make its claims auditable, and preserves the corpus's applicability and uncertainty limits. Equivalent authoritative sources and sound independently derived calculations are acceptable if they support the same population, frequency, observable, and regime. A source title, list of scenario labels, or recommendation without its substantive mechanism/evidence/limit is not coverage.

For scoring, mark each item **covered**, **partial**, or **omitted**. Report omissions against the candidate separately from **corpus gaps** below. Do not penalize a candidate for candidly retaining an unresolved empirical gap; penalize it if it turns that gap into a claimed measurement, calibrated distribution, or validated model.

## Material parity criteria (16)

1. **Define the propagation object and distinguish the two bands.** Coverage explains that the useful object is the received complex field/antenna response, while “surface wave,” Norton terms, space wave, diffraction and reflected rays are mathematical/regime-dependent decompositions, not independently identifiable modem packets. At 1 MHz, MF groundwave is plausible over useful regional distances; at 30 MHz, the P.368 endpoint does not imply arbitrary-path groundwave dominance, and antenna height, space wave, diffraction and other modes become central. Do not require a uniquely bound Zenneck wave. Equivalent treatment can use another correct total-field framework. **Locators:** Astra §§1–2 (`Astra` L5–34); 20 “Physical mechanisms” (`20` L14–65); 25 “Physical mechanisms,” esp. “What the word covers” (`25` L17–28).

2. **State the smooth-earth reference's domain and what its outputs mean.** A defensible baseline identifies P.368-10/LFMF assumptions (smooth homogeneous spherical earth, vertical polarization/reference radiator, refractivity treatment, frequency/height bounds) and its exclusions: terrain, vegetation/clutter, coastline, rough sea, actual antenna system and anomalous atmosphere. Distinguish P.368-10's linear refractivity profile/LFMF from superseded P.368-9/GRWAVE's exponential profile. Treat model output as a baseline, not a received-service guarantee. **Locators:** 20 §§A–B and “How the literature models” (`20` L68–138, L324–334); 25 §§A and model table (`25` L93–125, L271–287); 82 §§1–2 (`82` L21–119); Astra §1 (`Astra` L5–12).

3. **Make numerical quantities and radio reference planes auditable.** At least one calculation or table should connect field, basic transmission loss, power, antenna efficiency/reference gain, external noise and modem-relevant C/N0 or receiver threshold without mixing conventions. Preserve the approximately 6 dB low-terminal convention distinction (and 82's explicit 6.07 dB LFMF receive-power offset), its geometry dependence at 30 MHz, units, bandwidth, and assumptions. A different internally consistent set of reference planes is acceptable if fully defined and not double-counted. **Locators:** 20 “Units and conversions,” “Smooth homogeneous earth,” and “180 dB” (`20` L27–35, L82–138, L263–282); 25 conventions and §A (`25` L8–16, L93–125); 82 §§1–2 (`82` L21–119).

4. **Describe how ground electrical properties set attenuation, with frequency-specific regimes.** At 1 MHz include conductivity/permittivity, penetration depth (subsurface geology, moisture, temperature/freezing and water/salinity), atlas/local-property uncertainty, and why a topsoil sample or generic wet/dry switch is inadequate. At 30 MHz include the different role of land permittivity versus conductivity, sea conductivity/salinity, surface impedance, height scale relative to the roughly 10 m wavelength, and applicable polarization. Accept alternative well-supported parameterizations; require stated values and provenance. **Locators:** 20 “Ground constants” and §§C, parameter table (`20` L36–43, L139–151, L296–323); 25 “Surface impedance,” “Antenna height,” “Polarisation,” §§A/B (`25` L28–62, L93–143); Astra ground constants (`Astra` L29–34).

5. **Quantify the smooth-path baselines and their distance regimes.** The candidate should show a reproducible numerical progression or equivalent result for each band, tying distance/material/height/refractivity to field/loss. At 1 MHz retain the approximate 1/d near/short behavior, transition to curvature/residue-series decay and strong ground-constant sensitivity. At 30 MHz distinguish short flat-earth from curved-earth diffraction, sea versus land, and mention P.368's 30 MHz boundary. Exact corpus numbers are not mandatory when equivalent calculations are supplied, but an unquantified “more loss at longer distance” is insufficient. **Locators:** 20 §B and parameter table (`20` L82–138, L296–323); 25 §§1–3 and §A (`25` L19–49, L93–125); 82 §§2, 3.1, 4.1/4.5 (`82` L67–119, L122–139, L292–306, L414–430).

6. **Treat mixed land/sea propagation and coast transitions as a method question.** Include the land-to-sea recovery phenomenon and segment order; describe Millington's forward/reverse reciprocal amplitude construction as a practical approximation, with boundary/phase limits, and compare an alternative (e.g. equivalent-distance, integral, or full-wave) where useful. Use Nishioka et al.'s actual 1.242 MHz 19 km land + 71 km sea route as relative-profile evidence only: gain was unknown/fitted, and land fades were attributed rather than path-resolved. At 30 MHz include a height/coast diffraction or impedance-boundary alternative; do not infer exact coastal phase from amplitude stitching. Equivalent sources and a matched route calculation are acceptable. **Locators:** 20 “Mixed paths” and completion “Near-band coastal evidence” (`20` L52–58, L465–482); 25 coast/height discussion and Casciato resolution (`25` L81–86, L509–530); 82 1M-B/B2 and traceability (`82` L140–196, L450–465).

7. **Cover empirical evidence breadth without flattening distinct observables.** Parity requires a useful empirical spread: near-band MF mixed-path/urban or broadcast evidence; seasonal/diurnal/phase context from navigation or broadcast records; and upper-HF ocean/terrain observations. Report conditions and transfer limits. Examples in the corpus include 855/1200/1242 kHz field or reception trials, R-Mode/eLoran phase at lower frequencies, 16.188 MHz coastal one-way power, 25 MHz radar working range, 20/50 MHz NTIA spot terrain records, 28 MHz NTIA paths, and 32.55 MHz radar coexistence. Comparable evidence is fine; a bibliography alone is not. **Locators:** 20 §§D–I and completion §§1–3 (`20` L152–262, L465–566); 25 §§B–E and completion §§1–3 (`25` L126–245, L388–574); Astra evidence table (`Astra` L13–34).

8. **Separate spatial variation, temporal variation, seasonal shifts, and instrument/decoder outcomes.** Do not convert a mobile route's spatial fades into stationary time fading absent a motion model; keep medians, rms, mean deviations, percentile/site spread, and receiver decode outcomes distinct. Preserve contrary evidence: MF winter often strengthens despite frozen-soil half-space prediction; seasonal effects differ by geography; 16.188 MHz conductivity variation had no detectable seasonal power effect in one geometry; Halverson's 25 MHz *radar working range* varied with conductivity/wind/tide but is not one-way loss and had a capped/censored endpoint. **Locators:** 20 §§D/F, completion “Seasonal and phase evidence” (`20` L152–171, L186–198, L528–566); 25 “Sea paths,” “Variability,” K&E/Halverson limits (`25` L126–143, L190–205, L319–333, L509–574); Astra §2 (`Astra` L29–34).

9. **Handle 1 MHz layering, urban/terrain effects, and seasonal uncertainty conditionally.** At 1 MHz show that thin ice/slabs, frozen soil, buildings, vegetation and terrain are not interchangeable with a homogeneous half-space. Report model alternatives and their status (e.g. slab boundary-response versus complete curved-earth propagation), built-up losses/fades, and spatial scales relative to wavelength. Retain sign/causality contradictions and avoid asserting a universal seasonal correction. At 30 MHz, a few metres changes geometry materially; terrain, forest, edge diffraction and local clutter require their own evidence/model. **Locators:** 20 “Reactive surfaces,” “Mixed paths and terrain,” §§C/F, completion seasonal analysis (`20` L44–65, L139–151, L186–198, L528–550); 25 “Terrain, forest, buildings,” §C, NTIA archive (`25` L81–90, L144–189, L390–508); 82 1M-C2 and 30M-C/F (`82` L197–232, L350–391, L431–449).

10. **Represent night/twilight skywave at 1 MHz as a competing coherent mode with unresolved model observables.** Meaningful coverage separates daytime baseline from night return, gives path-dependent excess delay and reference-plane/polarization/antenna effects, and addresses coherent amplitude/phase cancellation, fades and Doppler. Preserve the P.1147 composite loop versus P.1321 per-mode level disagreement at 100–200 km as unresolved (not wholly “fixed” by one projection), plus sky/ground crossover and upper-distance daytime winter skywave as conditional estimates. Distinguish quiet-night diffuse fade rates from TID/terminator carrier shifts and from DRM test-channel assumptions. **Locators:** 20 “Sky-wave contamination,” §G, table, follow-up (`20` L59–65, L199–246, L296–323, L528–566); 82 1M-D (`82` L233–270); Astra §3 1 MHz (`Astra` L41–48).

11. **At 30 MHz distinguish real mode families and challenge any “180 dB” ranking.** Discuss at least the consequential candidates relevant to the claim (space/direct-reflected/diffracted, tropospheric ducting/superrefraction, troposcatter, sporadic-E/F skywave, meteor, aircraft, sea/ground backscatter, and interference), with distance, delay, Doppler, occurrence or calibration evidence where available. Acknowledge the striking 32 MHz +25 dB Hansen episode as a single geometry with bluff/calibration and attribution limits; 32.55 MHz radar shows coexisting modes but is not direct-path-normalized modem IQ. Keep derived mode budgets and unknown rates as bounds/sensitivity cases, not measured universal ranking. **Locators:** 25 “Atmosphere,” “What else arrives,” §§E–F and completion `Huyghebaert` (`25` L63–90, L206–245, L509–550); Astra source table (`Astra` L13–28); 82 30M-D/E and traceability (`82` L392–430, L450–536).

12. **Treat rough sea as at least two distinct questions: coherent mean loss and fluctuating scattered field.** A high-quality report can explain Barrick's spectrum-derived effective complex impedance and the wind/roughness validity limits, compare Phillips and Neumann–Pierson or an equivalent directional-spectrum alternative, and retain the several-dB disagreement. The mean-loss curve does not determine forward-path K-factor, covariance, Doppler PSD or phase statistics. Contrast Hansen's partly circular inferred-wind comparison, the one 16.188 MHz one-way record, and radar working-range results without conflating their metrics. **Locators:** 25 “Rough sea,” §§B/D, “Rough sea: what is actually supported” (`25` L71–80, L126–143, L190–205, L552–574); 82 30M-B/B2 (`82` L307–349); Astra evidence table (`Astra` L13–28).

13. **Compare modeling approaches by scope, not by name or sophistication.** Accept correctly delimited alternatives: LFMF/P.368 smooth-earth; Millington/equivalent-distance/integral mixed path; terrain integral equation (WAGNER/WAGSLAB), ITM/Longley–Rice, P.526 diffraction, empirical P.1546/P.1812/P.2001, parabolic-equation/FDTD, Barrick rough impedance, or mechanism-specific skywave/scatter models. Explain what each predicts and its measured/validated domain and limits. Do not imply a newer/full-wave solver is empirically better merely because it is more complex; the 2023 ocean record found simpler loss models could perform better. **Locators:** 20 “How literature models” (`20` L324–334); 25 model table and “What high-fidelity simulation would need” (`25` L271–318); Astra §4 (`Astra` L59–69).

14. **Give quantitative uncertainty, competing interpretations, and dependence—not a false worst-case distribution.** Use parameters/brackets with units, model/measurement uncertainty, access grade, and provenance; state which adverse entries are observed, modeled, graph-read, or assumed. Do not multiply independent marginal maxima into a natural joint event or use receiver failure to infer physical outage. Cover at least conductivity/class uncertainty at 1 MHz and sea state/refractivity/height/terrain/mode uncertainty at 30 MHz. **Locators:** 20 parameter table and contradictions/open gaps (`20` L296–408); 25 parameter table, critical-input map and contradictions (`25` L246–270, L319–387); 82 traceability, severity/dependence (`82` L450–536); Astra §2 (`Astra` L13–34).

15. **Connect the physics to an implementable native/appraisal plan and keep validation layers separate.** A substantive equivalent specifies actual scenario inputs, waveform/reference/noise planes, continuous delay/phase, source support and tails, model adapter/domain limits, and what is a synthetic sensitivity versus source-conditioned or measured-path replay. It separates numerical checks, empirical channel validation, native implementation, receiver acquisition/decode/completion and physical outage. Relevant 82 examples include 1M-A/B/B2/C/D/E and 30M-A/B/B2/C/D/E/F, the 1 W e.m.r.p. normalization, fixed Fa/Te reference case, sample-rate comparison and receiver completion controls; same details need not be copied if equivalent actual implementation implications are covered. **Locators:** 82 §§1–6 (`82` L21–119, L120–449, L450–574); Astra §§3–4 (`Astra` L35–69); 20/25 scope and modem comparisons (`20` L1–12, L360–379; `25` L1–16, L334–349).

16. **Name the evidence gaps that limit any empirical claim and define a usable validation record.** Corpus does not establish a calibrated 1–600 s, groundwave-dominant complex record near 1 MHz or a universal 1 MHz seasonal/phase distribution. Nearby 300 kHz navigation, 855/1200 kHz DRM, AM PSD/Doppler and receiver snapshots do not fill that exact need; PSD cannot reconstruct phase. At 30 MHz there is no matched low, below-horizon one-way sea complex/phase record paired with a raised visible receiver, measured sea spectrum/refractivity and uncensored outcomes; no calibrated 20–50 MHz groundwave Doppler/phase PSD or direct-relative 180 dB competitor ranking. A validation proposal should specify calibrated transmitter power/pattern, receive antenna/height, route/material/profile/time, complex samples and noise estimator, frequency/time reference, and uncensored nondetections, with held-out routes/dates. These are corpus gaps, not report omissions to “solve” by extrapolation. **Locators:** 20 completion/critical-input and gap disposition (`20` L458–600); 25 critical-input map, “Looked for and not found,” and gap dispositions (`25` L319–387, L509–607); 82 matched empirical validation and GW01–GW16 (`82` L501–535, L575–606); Astra evidence coverage/limits (`Astra` L70–103).
