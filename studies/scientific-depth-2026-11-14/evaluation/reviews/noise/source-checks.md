# Noise review source and calculation checks

## Direct source checks

- [ITU-R P.372-18 official status](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-P.372-18-202609-I): approved 2026-09-08, in force, official PDF 30,437,927 bytes, posted 2026-09-25. [Official P.372-18 scope summary](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/p/R-REC-P.372-18-202609-I!!SUM-HTM-E.htm) says 0.1 Hz–100 GHz and lists lightning, atmospheric gases/cloud/rain/Earth surface, galaxy and man-made sources. PDF contents remained inaccessible in the web reader; no current-v18 numeric clauses verified.
- [ITU-R P.372-17 official PDF](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/p/R-REC-P.372-17-202408-I!!PDF-E.pdf), §§1.2, 4.1, 6.1.1, 7.1. Direct text supports (f_a=p_n/(kT_0b)), (F_a=10\log_{10}f_a), 290 K reference, lossless-antenna plane, power relation, field conversions, APD need outside WGN-only cases, and the (F_{am}=c-d\log_{10}f_{MHz}) environmental-category formula. Table 1 values match the study script. Section 7 is an approximation for component noise-factor/quantile distributions; the study's sensitivity calculation does not execute that component combination.
- The same P.372-17 §§4.1/6.1.1 source explicitly limits ordinary categories to 0.3–250 MHz and describes quiet-rural curve D as ionospherically reflected man-made noise below (f_{xF2}), with Galactic curve E dominant above it. Hence 1 MHz unshielded Galactic is not a generic site floor. This confirms the report's stated caveat.
- [ITU-R SM.2449-1 official report](https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-r/opb/rep/R-REP-SM.2449-1-2024-PDF-E.pdf), Tables 30–31, pp. 59–60: at 1.65 MHz city P.372 vs Netherlands measurement levels are −37.56 vs −26.23 dBµA/m; residential −41.86 vs −32.34 dBµA/m. Report says the field values are converted using a 51.5 dB correction. Differences independently calculate to +11.33/+9.52 dB. These are converted magnetic-field-level data from scoped measurement projects, not noise density or universal correction.
- [Reuveni et al. (2010), full primary paper](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2009RS004336), §§2–4: Negev station; 81 m² orthogonal loops; 100 kHz, 16-bit recordings with one minute per 15 minutes across 386 days; calibrated frequency response; 75 Hz spectral-bin resolution and 4, 7.5, 10, 26 kHz Vd records. Paper defines Vd as RMS/average envelope ratio, finds Gaussian reference 1.05 dB, and distinguishes pooled annual Vd mixture from hour/season fits. This validates the report's use and limits, not MF/HF transfer.
- [ITU-R SM.1753-2](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/sm/R-REC-SM.1753-2-201209-I!!PDF-E.pdf), full 39-page official PDF: intended to harmonize comparable/reproducible noise measurement; separates WGN, impulse noise and single-carrier components; lays out equipment noise correction, 20% WGN method, APD, impulse/burst processing, uncertainty and limitations. This supports the report's measurement-procedure references but does not validate the candidate's stated 10 dB threshold specifically (not independently searched within the PDF in this review).
- [Analog Devices reciprocal-mixing article](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/technical-articles/specifications-and-measurement-of-local-oscillator-noise-in-integrated-circuit-base-station-mixers.html), 2003: describes blocker plus LO noise and reports a device-specific measurement from −134 dBm/Hz no blocker to −127 dBm/Hz with a 5 dBm blocker 25 MHz offset. This substantiates mechanism and an available bounded example, not project hardware limits.
