1. **Mechanisms and regimes — Supported.** The chapter distinguishes MF/HF atmospheric, man-made, Galactic, service-signal and receiver effects; treats VLF and microwave EME separately; and identifies foF2/fxF2, direction, geometry and receiver conditions.
2. **Literature discovery and original-source use — Partial.** The source ledger is unusually careful about direct access, dates, scope and failed access. I independently confirmed the official v18 identity/status and current scope, and read P.372-17, SM.1753-2, SM.2449-1, Reuveni et al. and the Analog Devices reciprocal-mixing source. The chapter still omits accessible foundational HF waveform/statistics work central to its APD/burst claims and has no current-v18 clause comparison. It is not an exhaustive search, and the 2026 standard limitation is disclosed.
3. **Alternatives and counterevidence — Partial.** The chapter compares P.372 to Dutch measurements and contrasts AWGN, structured interference and front-end mechanisms. It does not do a matched analysis of modern campaign methods, multiple HF waveform models or simultaneous/dependent component alternatives at comparable depth to the source corpus.
4. **Quantitative conventions and uncertainty — Supported boundary.** P.372 quantities, units, antenna assumptions, bandwidth and predecessor status are explicit; field-vs-power and measurement-vs-calculation distinctions are respected. Independent calculations reproduce the P.372-17 1/30 MHz table, the 1.65 MHz measured-vs-reference deltas, and the lunar main-beam outputs. No target chain exists, so dBm/Hz-to-dBFS, receiver NF and physical uncertainty remain correctly unassigned. Component-sum dependence, however, should be calculated before treating that branch as covered.
5. **Executed discriminators — Partial.** The delivered script runs and the published-value calculations are checkable; no new data product was analyzed, no MF/HF temporal-model alternative was calculated, and no noise-combination or receiver-transfer control was executed. Hardware qualification and pump execution were not necessary or authorized for this review.
6. **Decision relevance and next development discriminator — Supported boundary.** The main decision is appropriately conservative: keep current synthetic hooks for algorithm studies and defer environmental/RF claims until a calibrated plane and receiver exist. The proposed simultaneous input/ADC, termination, blocker, gain-state and Moon/blank-sky measurements are concrete and falsifiable. They do not substitute for the omitted desk evidence that is available now.

## Independent calculations and limitations

The branch's script ran successfully. I independently evaluated the official P.372-17 table equation (F_a=c-d\log_{10}(f_{MHz})), then (N_0=-173.975+F_a) dBm/Hz at 290 K and (P_{3kHz}=N_0+10\log_{10}(3000)). Outputs agree to 0.01 dB for all five categories at 1 and 30 MHz. I also reproduced the two 1.65 MHz Table 31 minus Table 30 differences: 11.33 dB city and 9.52 dB residential, in dBµA/m (not PSD). The 1 MHz unshielded galactic curve is 1.6 dB below the quiet-rural curve, but P.372-17 says curve D is ionospherically reflected man-made noise below (f_{xF2}), with Galactic noise becoming dominant above it; that is an adverse boundary to interpreting the unshielded curve as a measured 1 MHz site floor, and the report correctly flags it.

The first independent lunar recomputation attempt printed the P.372 table and MF field comparisons, then stopped with `NameError: name 'log' is not defined` before the lunar calculation; zero lunar calculations were completed by that attempt. I retained that failure and reran with the missing import. The corrected run reproduces the reported 0.6/1/3/6 m beam couplings (0.007/0.019/0.159/0.500) and antenna temperatures (1.0–1.9/2.7–5.3/22.3–44.6/70.1–140.1 K). This is a sensitivity estimate using a uniform 0.5° disk and Gaussian main beam, not measured antenna performance.

The Reuveni primary article confirms the reported 2007–08 Negev site, two orthogonal 81 m² loops, one minute per 15-minute interval, 100 kHz sampling and 75 Hz spectral bins; it explicitly distinguishes (V_d) impulsiveness from absolute PSD and reports that the all-year distribution needed two Gaussian components while hour/season distributions were fit separately. That evidence is valid for its measured bands/site and cannot validate an MF/HF or target-modem event process. ITU-R SM.2449-1 Table 30/31 is indeed a converted magnetic-field result at 1.65 MHz, so the report's unit and comparison caveats are material.
