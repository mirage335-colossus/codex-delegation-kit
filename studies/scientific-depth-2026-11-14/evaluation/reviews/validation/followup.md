## New evidence independently inspected

### Finite generator and fractional delay

I inspected the actual `src/triad_gaussian_fading.hpp` and `src/triad_random_process.hpp` implementations against `research/lead/generator_probe.cpp` and its retained stdout. The receive-grid Gaussian helper has the reported 8,193-tap guard and computes the finite-kernel coefficient covariance. The physical-time helper has 53 taps on a `16 B` knot clock, cubic interpolation, and finite-kernel variance normalization. The standalone probe compiles those existing headers, not the application or `StreamingChannel`; its 12 checks cover the rejection bound, selected-grid covariance agreement, same physical-time query invariance, and a coarse deterministic-root check. The root check is explicitly not proof of IID roots or field fidelity. The accompanying independent Gram calculation integrates over knot origin and reports both average ideal-ACF error and phase sensitivity (about `1.1e-4` error at `B·lag=.45`). Its per-second costs are limited arithmetic estimates, not measured throughput.

The validation continuation's `fractional_delay_probe.py/.txt` independently re-expresses the formula at `src/triad_simulation_stream.cpp:193–223`. It checks complex tones, impulse support, zero-history start, a deliberately broken chunk-reset counterfactual, and linear gain preservation. The largest plotted tone error (`−1.65 dB`, about `13.8°`) is at `.45` cycles/sample, outside the nominal 225-Hz occupied band at 960 samples/s; near `.125` cycles/sample it is negligible. This is a finite-kernel edge result, not evidence of a target-profile failure. Precursor and finite support depend on the prepared waveform's band/ramp and capture guard. The broken-reset comparison is not current `StreamingChannel` behavior: source inspection shows state and history persist across reads. The probe supplies the previously absent, quantitative interpolation/support boundary, but not full source-to-channel tail integration.

The native branch's additional existing-fixture audit gives a more useful HF sample-clock bound: `tests/test_gui_robust_live.cpp::late_affine_clock_support` constructs a Helper-on 6,000-sample/s fixture (36-Hz fine chip rate, 225-Hz DATA, ten added symbols, `hf-worst` and `each-65`). Source-derived spectral/Helper arithmetic gives a 325-Hz conservative DATA support, a 21-ms delay and `S≈360.52 Hz`; an accepted high Helper tone is at most 2,500 Hz under the 3,000-Hz Nyquist cap, requiring `Fs>5,921.04 Hz`, so the fixture's 6,000 Hz clears that derived bound. The corresponding ring estimate is about 191 complex values / 3,056 bytes per source lane and two-ray interpolation is at most 192,000 tap contributions/s/geometry. These are source and fixture calculations, not a run of the fixture, proof that either Helper tone is selected/certified, or an actual physical receiver setting. They narrow one realistic HF arithmetic case while leaving other regimes and the target profile conditional. See `research/native/source-ledger.md` §10 and `research/native/chapter.md` §§9–10.

### Public observations and reference comparator

I inspected the expanded HF regime and noise chapters, the posted numeric outputs, and the HF-reference analysis/source ledger. The HF literature includes measured conditional path spreads and event time histories; the newer Java NVIS account reports published CIR values but no scheduled-slot denominator, so its nominal 672 quarter-hour slots cannot become an availability estimate. Noise continuation inspects a measured dawn HF field PDF, a historical atmospheric APD, and a regional thresholded occupancy statistic, carefully distinguishing each band's/site/time/measurement statistic from modem slot loss. These are actual primary observables, not merely generic advice.

The strongest new direct source-observable comparison is `research/hf-reference/reference_checks.py/.txt` and `chapter.md`. Its inputs are hash-identified files from ITU-R SG3 repository revision `82017594a1c6cacfaa7e86954c4ae7b3a5825a3d`: D1 route/month metadata, measured hourly field values, and headerless posted comparison predictions. The script verifies matching `(ID, year, month)` keys and 24-hour row widths, excludes measurement sentinel `99` without converting it to zero, retains large errors, and computes matched residuals and strata. I reran it with `python3 -I`; it exited successfully and reproduced the retained summary. The result is 16,268 observed cells, 22,444 missing cells, 181 route-frequency IDs, overall `−0.06 dB` mean error and `10.23 dB` sample SD/RMSE, with 4,942 absolute differences at least 10 dB. The same route/month/hour comparison across 6.4, 12.7, and 17.1 MHz preserves frequency-dependent residuals. This is a useful matched planning-field observable comparison and exposes denominator/stratification issues.

The comparator is not a held-out P.533 validation: P.533's empirical loss was calibrated/standardized partly against D1; the upstream comparison script says posted predictions may come from ITURHFProp or REC533 and the posted table does not identify the executable/version per row. The data are monthly reference field tables, not fine-time complex waveforms, packet outcomes, nor an availability denominator. The full source ledger records repository pin, eight file hashes, data matching, a failed DNS clone and its authorized connector route, and the producer ambiguity. No claim of a new pinned-executable run or a modem channel fit is warranted.

### Matched controls and admitted pilot design

The validation continuation now gives a five-regime matrix covering sub-9-kHz antenna/coupling, approximately 1-MHz and 30-MHz groundwave separately, HF skywave, and passive 5.8-GHz EME. Each row states reference instruments/planes and measured observables, matched near-term checks and negative controls, holdout/access conditions, and what does or does not qualify. It correctly separates an instrument/source measurement, source-observable validation, complete-receiver comparison, and physical-population claim. The plan explicitly says which unprovided calibration/profile inputs block capture; it does not mislabel a future receiver run as performed.

The corrected pilot budgets four records per regime (20 total) and 40 candidate/baseline replays. Its exact parametric tail now follows source: `tail_samples = ceil(delay_tail_real) + perturb_tail + tx_interpolation_tail + frontend_tail`, with the defined maximum-delay, selective-tail, 16-tap interpolation lookahead and frontend low-pass terms; it also includes source-time/timing extension and capture lead/trail. The source's final count is `max(natural_total, minimum_capture_samples)`. In the live message path, `minimum_capture_samples` is the absolute public-calendar stop endpoint, chosen to cover two scored quiet slots. The corrected pilot formula takes the maximum of the source-composed natural count and that endpoint, avoiding a quiet-slot double count. I independently checked this against `pumpModem/src/triad_simulation_stream.cpp:530–546` and `src/live.cpp:782–790`. The code's `+64` history ring is memory, not output-tail time. Each record's `T[d,j]` and `N[d,j]=ceil(Fs[d] T[d,j])` must be computed from the actual prepared profile before capture, and one complex-double lane plus mask costs `17 N` bytes. The initial 652-second / 507.4-MiB illustration is explicitly superseded because its arbitrary 1/2/3-second tails omitted these terms. The corrected active intervals alone sum to 620 s; at the hypothetical 48 ksample/s that lower bound is 29,760,000 samples / 505,920,000 bytes (482.5 MiB) per lane before any lead, tails, quiet slots or observed longer decay. The plan sets a 1.25-GiB total and 16-MiB metadata cap and rejects capture when profile/tail/rate/lane/storage inputs are unknown or over cap. Exact total sample count and receiver-version seconds therefore remain unavailable until those inputs exist; the plan does not invent them. It keeps CPU throughput qualification separate. The native Helper-on 6,000-sample/s fixture supplies a source-grounded HF arithmetic point, but selected tone/spectral certificate and sample/capture plan remain unobserved. This is a conditional, resource-bounded design; no capture or replay has been admitted or executed.
