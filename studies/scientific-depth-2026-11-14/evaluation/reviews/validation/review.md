## Independent source and calculation checks

### First-party standards and project contract

- The official [ITU-R P.368-10 body](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/p/R-REC-P.368-10-202208-I%21%21PDF-E.pdf), Annex 1, §§1 and opening conditions, confirms the stated boundary: 10 kHz–30 MHz; generally use when ionospheric reflections are negligible; not for receivers well above Earth; smooth homogeneous spherical Earth; transmit and receive antennas on or near the surface; short vertical monopole reference, 1 kW, vertical far-field component. It is a reference method, not a path-specific physical calibration. This supports the chapter's §3.1 caveat.
- The official [ITU-R P.533-14 body](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/p/R-REC-P.533-14-201908-I%21%21PDF-E.pdf), §§7–10 (printed pp. 22–24), confirms monthly median SNR and decile context, then defines digital circuit reliability using signal time/frequency windows and a service-specific required SNR. It does not supply a fine-time channel trajectory or modem episode population. The chapter accurately restricts transfer.
- ITU's current [P.372-18 record](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-P.372-18-202609-I/en) says it was approved 2026-09-08 and is in force. The official [summary HTML](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/p/R-REC-P.372-18-202609-I%21%21SUM-HTM-E.htm) is accessible and states the broad 0.1 Hz–100 GHz radio-noise scope and source classes. Opening the current 30,437,927-byte PDF returned HTTP 400 “Content length is too large.” Thus detailed P.372-18 provisions remain unverified here; the participant correctly attributes its detailed clauses to superseded P.372-15 and says to recheck the current body. The summary HTML is a small relevant route that should be listed in the source ledger, but it does not close the detailed-body gap.
- NIST's [TN 1297 §4](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-4-type-b-evaluation-standard-uncertainty) supports using prior measurements, instrument/calibration information, reference data, empirical process models and check standards in uncertainty assessment. This supports the chapter's need for an instrument-specific budget; it cannot supply the missing radio fixture values.
- Current project documentation is consistent with the participant's abstract contract: `docs/robust-current.md` describes bounded native complete-receiver processing, public hypotheses, invalid-sample erasure behavior, and two fully scored quiet slots for completion; `docs/channel-models.md` says the normalized sampled channel and synthetic assumptions do not establish physical-device qualification. The participant labels the <$1 processing-second/input-second point as owner-supplied and a capacity threshold, not a link-reliability target. I did not independently benchmark it or inspect underlying receiver source for runtime behavior.

### Recomputed numerical evidence

The separately retained desk check reproduces the participant's zero-failure one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson upper bounds: `9.503385286%` at 0/30, `2.951304961%` at 0/100, `0.993608194%` at 0/300 and `0.299124955%` at 0/1000. Inverting the same formula gives 299, 2,995 and 29,956 independent episodes for upper limits below 1%, 0.1% and 0.01%. These calculations are valid only for independent Bernoulli episodes from a defined target population, a limitation stated in the chapter.

I independently recomputed the finite-n AR(1) mean-variance design effect by closed geometric sums, rather than rerunning the submitted loop. At `rho=0.9`, effective sizes are 5.81394 for 100 windows, 53.13496 for 1,000 and 526.81488 for 10,000; the participant's values agree to rounding. This is a useful adverse IID-window illustration for a mean, not a failure-rate correction or a channel law. The 290 K white thermal conversions also reproduce: 2.4 kHz gives `−140.17789 dBm` and a 33.80211 dB `C/N0` to SNR offset; 12 kHz gives `−133.18819 dBm` and 40.79181 dB. Shared additive dB calibration error cancels from a paired difference algebraically; it does not establish absolute calibration.

These are correct, auditable illustrations. They do not replace the frozen reference's finite Gaussian/OU covariance and sampled-generator checks, a measured channel observable, or a complete-receiver comparison.

## Six domain-level depth checks

1. **Mechanisms/regimes/observables — Partial.** The method clearly distinguishes physical power planes, noise, interference, missing input, fades and service outcomes, and warns against transferring standards across disjoint regimes. It delegates VLF/EME/groundwave specifics to other science owners, but the validation branch itself does not trace a physical channel through actual sampled dynamics.
2. **Foundational and recent primary discovery — Partial.** The ledger is strong on official standards and foundational inference/metrology sources, and it labels access limits. The current P.372-18 official status/summary is available, while detailed body content remains blocked. The search does not cover recent primary simulator/channel-validation results comparable to the reference's generator/replay literature.
3. **Alternatives and counterevidence — Partial.** It compares measurement versus synthesis, reference standards versus transient episodes, and paired versus unpaired receiver analysis. It lacks implementation-level generator alternatives, error tradeoffs and a counterexample in the sampled-channel domain.
4. **Quantitative interpretation — Supported for the worked statistical/metrology examples.** Units and assumptions are explicit; CP, AR(1), bandwidth, calibration covariance and censoring interpretations are mostly disciplined. The new independent arithmetic agrees. This does not constitute empirical channel calibration.
5. **Executed discriminators — Partial.** The independent calculations are executed and the official P.368/P.533 clauses were checked. No public captured channel, replay, model-output discrepancy or finite generator approximation was analyzed; no receiver run was authorized or performed. Some relevant capture evidence might not exist, but the accessible-data search and exact route failure need to be established before treating this as a closed boundary.
6. **Decision and next development discriminator — Partial.** The proposed controls are decision-relevant and usually state what a disagreement would mean. They are not prioritized or costed against sample count, workload and available resources; exact missing inputs and an admitted small next measurement are not supplied.
