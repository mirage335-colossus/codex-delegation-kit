## Six research-depth dimensions

1. **Mechanisms, regimes, observables:** Good native treatment of channel stationarity, multiple path state, delay/phase, colored Gaussian versus impulsive noise, front-end ordering, valid versus missing observations, and configured versus unknown receiver calendar. It correctly says physical branch evidence must set covariance and distributions. It does not propose that one statistical channel transfer across VLF, groundwave, HF and EME.
2. **Foundational/recent discovery:** The first-pass native source ledger documents metered discovery and primary access for foundational channel/SOS, fractional delay and colored-noise work plus current 3GPP and ITU scopes. I independently opened Pop & Beaulieu (2001) author full text and the official 2026-09 ETSI/3GPP V19.5.0 report. Pop & Beaulieu's paper identifies nonstationarity and a random-phase correction in the classical Jakes construction; it is a counterexample to choosing classical Jakes by habit, not proof every randomized finite SOS is unsuitable. The current 3GPP TDL report is an implementation precedent within its declared 0.5–100-GHz model scope, not validation of low-HF or EME. A second review open of Zheng & Xiao returned a cache-miss fetch error. I preserve the first-pass ledger's inspected-paper claim but do not claim an independent second inspection of that full text.
3. **Alternatives/counterevidence:** The report compares exact OU against Jakes/Bessel target, randomized SOS, shaped innovations, direct-FIR/polyphase/Farrow interpolation and compact colored-noise whitening. It names where exact OU or Gaussian colored assumptions are appropriate and where they fail; it also notices the classical SOS counterexample. The native report remains thin on concrete replay versus measured stochastic versus geometry/physics/hybrid/surrogate comparisons; those alternatives exist in Fable `70`/Astra `60`, but current per-domain inputs and decision consequences are not joined to an adopted native choice.
4. **Quantitative conventions and uncertainty:** The AR(1) check matches lag-one coefficient and finds at normalized `f_DΔt=0.05`, lag-10 Jakes correlation `−0.304242` versus AR(1) `+0.780142`, absolute difference `1.084384`; at lag 20 difference is `0.388345`. The long-epoch phase example gives an exact 0.2-cycle fractional reference but binary64's fractional phase is 0, with a 2,048-cycle product ULP. Both artifacts reproduced exactly when rerun. The source default DATA-rate gate also checks arithmetically. These are clear mathematical counterexamples with limits, not channel measurement or observed product defect. The report's interpolation ops are transparent but do not give complete-channel occupancy/resource budgets.
5. **Performed discriminators:** Reran both retained native calculator scripts; inspected current source formulas and default geometry; independently computed the omitted long-VLF/wide-EME scale contrast; opened the adverse Pop-Beaulieu primary paper and inspected the official 3GPP report scope. No app/data capture, product build or receiver experiment was performed or required in this role. The missing VLF/EME calculation was feasible and remains a gap in the fresh artifact even though this review now quantifies it.
6. **Decision and next development discriminator:** Strong staged falsifiers: compare ACF/PSD/LCR/AFD for OU/SOS/filter on independent seeds/windows; compare interpolation against a high-precision reference over occupied band and delay velocity; evaluate public receiver outcomes separate from hidden oracle truth; measure complete baseline/corrected p95/p99, queue and memory with matched inputs. Make one explicit current decision: keep existing OU as an exponential-control only, and implement no new gain law absent calibrated/selected covariance. Before a new channel experiment, calculate complete composite band and resource admission, then use low-dimensional models only with exact-reference comparison where support needs it. The report's later hardware and empirical tests are properly proposed rather than fabricated.

## Desk arithmetic and decision consequence

The reproduced output in `resource-crosscheck.out` is:

- Default modem geometry: 36-Hz inner rate and 10 added symbols yield 60-Hz fine chip rate; 75-Hz carrier spacing; 225-Hz nominal DATA width. Current geometry requires at least 240 Hz and strictly more than 247.5 Hz; default 960 Hz passes.
- VLF physical-time example: 421.875 hours = 1,518,750 seconds. At 960 samples/s that is 1.458 billion sample instants. Materializing all IQ plus validity in memory would be an illustrative 24.786 GB if complex double occupies 16 B and the mask 1 B/sample; this is not the production streaming footprint, and no complete message is asserted to use this exact rate/profile.
- EME literal-grid example: 256×512 cells × 48,000 samples/s × 1,800 s = 11,324,620,800,000 cell-sample products before receiver work. At assumed (not measured) product rates of 10^8, 10^9, and 10^10/s this simple product count would take 31.46, 3.146, and 0.315 hours. It omits all per-product complex arithmetic multiplicity, coefficient preparation, memory behavior and receiver work; it cannot predict runtime.

These scales support two different efficiency moves: preserve VLF physical time and stream bounded windows/low-rate latent state without resetting the process; avoid literal full-grid EME as a routine first pass by checking a low-dimensional kernel against a small exact-grid reference and held-out observables. Neither move is a fidelity claim until its matched observable is checked. This reviewer calculation exposes the gap; it should be added to the live research before NATIVE-08 is considered supported.
