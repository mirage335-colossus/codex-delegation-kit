2. **Foundational and recent primary discovery:** Strong, with bounded access. 3. **Alternatives and counterevidence:** Strong. Coherent, GLRT/projection, marginalization, causal tracking, mixtures/switching, noncoherent, robust residual and reduced-score options are conditioned on priors, covariance, coherence, and complexity. Counterevidence includes TEC-phase omission, tracking estimation error, bad Helper narrowing, and high-dimensional multiple-comparison bias.
4. **Quantitative conventions and uncertainty:** Strong for the desk scope. Width definitions (`2σ`, RMS versus support), reference-plane/noise assumptions, equal-marginal covariance, KL, independence assumptions, and the toy model's limitations are stated. Measurements are selected and often censored; no joint population law is inferred.
5. **Performed discriminators and failures:** Sufficient for a bounded research-depth study, not product qualification. The saved three-carrier calculation and finite model-identification simulation, independently repeated covariance/KL and null arithmetic, and companion generator kernel/header checks answer accessible model questions. The error and correction history is retained. Neither native receiver runtime nor physical captures were supplied/performed, a stated scope boundary.
6. **Decision and next discriminator:** Strong except for RX-09 cost ledger.
