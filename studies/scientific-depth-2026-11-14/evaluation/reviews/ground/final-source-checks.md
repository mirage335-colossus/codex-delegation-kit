- Primary checks: Nishioka et al. 2014 full 5-page J-STAGE PDF; ITU-R P.1147-4 official PDF; Bourlier & Kubické 2011 full 9-page author-hosted journal PDF; Halverson et al. 2017 full 16-page paper; Kirincich & Emery 2023 publisher article. See chapter/source-ledger lines cited in `final-review.md` and the direct URLs in the chapter.
- P.1147 source observation: annual median of half-hourly **skywave field strength**, measured by a vertically oriented ground-level loop in the great-circle vertical plane; scope 150–1700 kHz and 50–12,000 km. It is not a minute-resolved complex echo or an LFMF connector-power value. The cross-domain 419-km output is cited from `research/hf-reference/chapter.md:11-25`; this review verified official input/time/receiver conventions and did not independently recompute its tabular value.
- Nishioka source observation: 1.242-MHz vertical broadcast; received carrier power every 2.5 s along the coast/island path; land/sea inputs and zero heights are printed. The authors lacked receive monopole gain and the received-power/incident-field relationship, so they fit theory to measured sea-region values and claim relative-envelope agreement plus coast recovery. This means relative spatial comparison is admissible; absolute path-loss residual or calibrated phase is not.
- Bourlier & Kubické primary output: 3–30-MHz vertical line-source and 1-D rough/mixed-surface setup. Their 10 realization ensemble and ≤1-dB error compare their roughness approximation against their own BMIA-CAG numerical rough-surface reference, not against smooth sea or field observations. The wind-variation 800-km difference and 1,000-km island recovery are separate model quantities. Earth curvature is neglected; their long-range result does not validate a short 30-MHz receiver path.
- Halverson primary observable: 25-MHz HFR radial-current **working range**, defined as farthest radial-velocity solution; the 1.6-year time series responds to conductivity, wind/sea state, and tides. It is not one-way attenuation/SNR. Kirincich & Emery publisher article confirms the 8-month one-way received-power observations from shore to an offshore tower and island and differing environmental relationships at those two positions. Exact paper stats used in the chapter are author-reported from its full-text/source-ledger review and were not reprocessed in this review.
- C/N0 input category caveat: P.372-17 PDF retrieval failed with 404 during this review. The chapter transparently uses predecessor-edition conditional category curves and omits atmospheric noise at 1 MHz, ionospheric suppression of galactic noise and site RFI. Input values correspond to the source/output in the fresh noise branch; this review validates the receiver arithmetic conditional on those values, not the full P.372-17 source extraction.
