## Six research-depth dimensions

1. **Mechanisms, regimes and observables — supported at the declared conditional scope.** The combined work distinguishes measured local radar echoes, conditional whole-disk EME shape, common shift versus relative spread, monostatic versus materially bistatic geometry, antenna-plane power versus noise temperature, and propagation absence versus receiver validity. Frequency, angular law, pointing, epoch, polarization, visibility and receiver window dependence are carried through the scenario. No actual epoch/site is asserted.
2. **Literature discovery and primary evidence — supported with an explicit access/transfer boundary.** The addendum records source-specific inspection and failed equation-asset access rather than citing inaccessible content (`source-ledger.md:7–16`). I independently checked Sultan-Salem and Tyler's original 2006 publisher PDF (abstract, §§1–2, lines 0–75 and 130–193): the description of Hagfors' limits and the two RCS averages agrees with the implementation's stated boundary. The chapter also preserves the earlier Sun et al. 2020 distinction between a predicted gradient and observed widths, and the mismatch in band/geometry of Evans and Campbell. The 48-request search is explicitly described as limited; it does not prove general nonexistence of target-band calibrated data.
3. **Alternatives and adverse evidence — supported.** Distinct projected, depth, diffuse, Hagfors, fitted-Hagfors, mixture, polarization and phase models are compared. Adverse angular-law transfer, finite-area/energy limitations, held-out centroid errors, D2 covariance error, beam-offset asymmetry, reduced wet capture and blocked path are retained. No model is selected as a physical lunar truth.
4. **Quantitative interpretation — supported for conditional calculations.** Surface/projected measures, RCS normalization, power versus field, units, frequency and geometry scaling, receiver plane, beam and atmospheric terms, estimator definitions, joint windows, uncertainty variants and quadrature behavior are stated. The integration ledger records the failed early normalization and 0.06 Hz convergence target; final observed convergence is bounded to 0.113 Hz outer frequency quantile and 0.029 percentage points in window power, not silently upgraded to product accuracy (`source-ledger.md:18–27`).
5. **Executed discriminators — supported as desk evidence, with no physical qualification implied.** Author-run 720,000/2.88-million-point calculations and analytical checks are recorded with command and limits. The global adjudicator independently replayed the default angular and atmosphere outputs byte-identically; that replay is coordinator-supplied evidence, not a command I ran. The original 2006 primary check independently confirms the central Hagfors caveat. No pump build, receiver trial, antenna calibration, field capture, site-specific weather replay or runtime benchmark occurred or is claimed.
6. **Decision relevance and next discriminator — supported.** The alternatives map to a receiver's specific delay/frequency capture and fading observables, and to scalar control, bounded synthetic paths or calibrated replay through the existing native interface. Falsifying observations are concrete: known-waveform, co/cross-polar target-band capture with calibrated antenna/noise and held-out windows, plus repeated coherent IQ/reference for covariance. Actual station pattern, ephemeris and weather remain inputs only if a later case declares them.
