# Prospective evaluation of research depth and development uptake

The comparison covers the combined technical work in pumpModem’s high-fidelity and resilience `_ref/` collections. The inventories and domain maps in this directory identify the source corpus and collapse repeated treatments of the same question. The corresponding `_appraisal/` documents calibrate what a source-supported recommendation, an executed check and a later qualification result look like. They also expose unresolved handoff questions. The newer appraisal results do not become a requirement to rerun pumpModem or a substitute for the research in the reference corpus.

## Comparison design

Observe one prospective, broad development case under the source-checkout guidance. The initial development assignment should describe its real engineering objective, regimes, existing interfaces and deliverable in ordinary terms. It should leave the form and depth of evidence gathering to the owner and coordinator. Preserve the actual assignment wording, the guidance supplied and the first owner response or request. Report whether research was requested because the owner identified decision-changing knowledge gaps, or was explicitly induced by the coordinator after the fact. The subsequent lead and domain assignments may explicitly request research.

The initial owner, domain researchers and synthesis lead may inspect the allowed project code and current canonical documentation. Keep the curated reference and appraisal, earlier study results, evaluator maps, criteria and expected conclusions outside their supplied context and procedural read scope. Give participants enough neutral task detail to address the full operating envelope, including distinct frequencies, geometries, receiver scope and implementation constraints. Do not conceal engineering inputs needed to make a decision. Record this as a procedural boundary unless independently enforced. Keep reviewers independent of the authors and document their access to participant work and the comparison corpus. This is a demonstration of the observed workflow in one case, with no treatment-effect, superiority, incidence or generalization estimate.

Freeze the criteria before participant research begins. Preserve the version, source identifiers and date of the freeze and retain initial results, including failures and access gaps. Use the same criteria for an authorized follow-up and final adjudication. Keep availability changes or corrections explicit and retain the original dispositions. Freeze the final research and synthesis inputs before evaluating later uptake. If the exercise includes new observations for the uptake stage, establish their provenance and preserve exactly what the new owner received.

## Research-depth requirements

Judge each matrix row by the substance of the evidence. The matrix’s source locators are evaluator aids, with named models, measurements and calculations serving as examples of comparable questions. Accept a materially equivalent answer with different studies, estimators or model choices if it addresses the same engineering decision and regime with comparable empirical and quantitative support. Treat a consequential contradiction as an opportunity to test interpretation or assumptions. Following the original conclusion earns no credit by itself. Publication date, primary access, the reported observable and its applicability matter more than citation or word counts.

Apply the following depth checks independently in each domain.

1. Map mechanisms, regimes, observables and operating conditions, including physical absences or failures where relevant. Identify which conclusions vary by frequency, geometry, time, instrumentation, severity or receiver capability.
2. Show actual literature discovery across foundational approaches and recent relevant primary work, checking original passages, tables, data or official procedures. Retain queries and useful backward or forward source links, publication and observation dates, material exclusions and failed access. If a recent source does not support a material decision, record what the search covered and what the available work establishes.
3. Compare materially different viable alternatives with applicability, cost and failure conditions. Check equivalence against known formulations and current project behavior when consequential. Preserve counterevidence, plausible alternate explanations or conditional behavior, along with the observation that would resolve them.
4. Show quantitative interpretation that can be independently checked. Keep observable definitions, units, reference plane, width or estimator conventions, sampling and calibration limits, conditions, uncertainty and any normalization explicit. Distinguish observations, standardized controls, derivations, extrapolations and proposed choices.
5. Execute independent desk-research discriminators for material uncertainties where accessible. This includes source-backed calculations, small research-only numerical checks and examination or analysis of appropriate public products or comparable primary observables. A worked paper example, calculation or data check must report its inputs, method, observed output, limit and any failed comparison. A future test description alone does not establish this depth. Do not require a specific paper, archive, official binary or absolute result if another accessible discriminator answers the same decision. Retain what remains uncertain and distinguish desk work from experiments requiring access, pumpModem execution or hardware.
6. Link findings to decisions and to a concrete next development discriminator. State what to measure or run, what result favors each alternative, what would change the decision and what conditions still prevent transfer. Cover reusable detail and a coherent cross-domain synthesis linking scientific conclusions to the engineering objective.

The primary comparison is against the deduplicated reference research, including its performed analysis to the extent needed for the questions it settles. No single attractive calculation, domain or numerical score can compensate for a missing major branch. A prose-only review that leaves feasible material empirical or quantitative discriminators at proposal stage remains partial even if its sources are excellent. No common requirement fixes study length, mandates chosen papers, equates more model complexity with better science or demands a calibrated answer when the relevant observable is unidentifiable.

For every row record the participant artifact and locator, the inspected primary or project evidence, the comparison to the reference’s decision and limits, and a disposition:

- **Supported** when the result meets the scoped question and satisfies its applicable depth checks.
- **Supported boundary** when the original physical or empirical result is unresolved or inapplicable under the declared conditions and the participant independently demonstrates the limitation with inspected evidence, pursues available discriminators and provides an appropriate conditional conclusion or concrete next input.
- **Partial** when a material branch, condition, available discriminator, quantitative context or decision link remains unaddressed.
- **Unsupported** when the cited evidence does not substantiate a consequential assertion, or the report claims observation, calibration, equivalence or qualification it did not establish.
- **Outside declared conditions** for a scoped-conditional row only, with the declared scope, evidence and reason it cannot alter the engineering decision. Inconvenient cases, uncertainty and unavailable evidence do not by themselves put a row outside scope.

An original open evidence gap can qualify as a supported boundary when the response meets that standard. The same label cannot excuse an unperformed feasible check or conceal a new gap. Independently verify the decisive source and numerical claims and an adverse or boundary example in each domain. Resolve feasible material gaps with the existing research owner under a prospective allocation before declaring the domain covered. If a source, budget, tool or other limit leaves a material gap, report the affected rows, status and useful next check. A domain meets the declared comparison when its essential rows and applicable conditional rows are supported or supported boundaries and all domain-level depth checks are satisfied. Require this judgment for all domains and reconcile contradictions spanning domains in the synthesis.

## Development uptake

Use a fresh engineering owner after the research freeze. Give the owner a normal development or audit task using the newly produced reusable research and a compact handoff pointing into it, plus the applicable current source and new observations. Withhold comparison answers and evaluation criteria. Supply raw observations with definitions, inputs and limitations, leaving their disposition open. Disclose any constructed diagnostic evidence and score only the resulting decisions. Do not represent the diagnostic as physical, field, receiver or capacity evidence.

The plan must retrieve the new research and inspect the decisive current entry points. Across the covered domains trace material evidence through mechanism and applicability to a current development choice, affected code or experiment, and discriminating check with expected and falsifying outcomes. Check for existing capability or mathematical equivalence, conditional adoption, and a conflicting or weak observation that changes or delays a commitment. Distinguish observed code behavior from proposed changes and executed research discriminators from planned production checks. Preserve admissible public inputs, state and result scopes, native contracts and representative resource limits. An accepted alternative plan is possible when the evidence and constraints support it. A report that repeats the research without applying it to the actual next decision is insufficient.

Independent uptake review should trace each material decision to its research passages, inspected code or experiment contract and the supplied observation. Report which observations change a decision and how the owner carries forward unresolved gaps. The scope is scientific research and a development plan. PumpModem edits, builds, receiver trials and hardware qualification remain outside this study.

## Reporting and resource record

Report the observed trigger, independent per-domain judgments and cross-domain synthesis, and fresh-owner uptake as separate outcomes. Record instrumented web requests including failures, applicable allowances or admitted extensions, and any independently observed deviations or uninstrumented work that affect interpretation. Tool telemetry establishes recorded operations and limits of the mechanism. It does not establish scientific coverage, genuine source understanding or compliance of activity outside that mechanism. Cite both the material gaps and the supported conclusions. Full requested demonstration requires all three outcomes at their declared expected scope.
