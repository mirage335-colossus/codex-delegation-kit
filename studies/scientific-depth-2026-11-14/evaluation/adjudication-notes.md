The tiny actual-helper probe reports 12 passing checks, including admitted covariance examples, a rejected tap-count case, and common physical-time samples. Its companion equal-marginal Markov calculation shows why an availability baseline needs temporal run and recovery observations. These are usable research results for finite-process correctness and independent observables. They exercise no complete physics-to-I/Q channel or decoder. Cite them together with the physical-to-sampled derivation, a causal-support discriminator, and physical/source-observable validation evidence for the relevant conclusions. For the next plan, keep matched receiver tests prospective. Separate validation of a source observable from claims of replay sufficiency, complete decoding and capacity. A case can be designed and costed before a build, an available calibrated capture or an approved campaign. Its future outcome stays unobserved until execution is authorized and recorded. The lack of a product build or receiver outcome, by itself, supplies no grounds for a Partial rating in this study. An unperformed feasible desk discriminator or a materially incomplete comparison design still does.
