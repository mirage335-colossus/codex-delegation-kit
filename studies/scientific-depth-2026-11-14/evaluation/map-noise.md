## Material content criteria

Use these as substantive coverage and reasoning criteria. A candidate can satisfy one in its own sound organization; exact phrasing, paper counts, and a citation-per-claim quota are not requirements. Depth means explaining the relevant mechanism/evidence and its practical boundary, not merely naming it.

1. **Separate physical source classes from received interference.** Explain atmospheric lightning, aggregate man-made noise and galactic background as distinct sources, and distinguish them from co-/adjacent-channel services and identifiable emitters that a receiver still experiences. Preserve that P.372 excludes service signals/single sources while they can dominate practical HF conditions. Locator: Fable-5.2/50, “Physical mechanisms,” especially “Three external sources plus interference,” and §§8–10; Astra-6/50 “Findings” and 1–30 MHz implications.

2. **Describe mechanisms in their domain.** Connect lightning activity and propagation/ionospheric absorption to MF/HF frequency and day/night behavior; connect aggregate household/electrical sources and their local coupling/ground path to man-made fields; describe Galactic synchrotron and direction/ionosphere limits. Do not turn a frequency-specific or geography-specific mechanism into a universal scalar. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Physical mechanisms,” “Measured and reported values” §§2–3, and regional synthesis; Astra-6/50 “1 MHz and 30 MHz ground wave; 1–30 MHz sky wave.”

3. **Use standards as bounded baselines, with version and provenance.** A strong account identifies what current P.372-18 contributes and what it inherits, including old atmospheric maps and man-made medians, edition changes, its planning/reference role, and lack of target-site or waveform guarantee. Distinguish normative text, code behavior, report, and secondary interpretation. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Measured and reported values” §1 and “Combination rule”; Astra-6/50 N01 and annotated reference.

4. **Compare modern measurements without flattening disagreement.** Bring forward material counterevidence (e.g., Dutch dense-residential elevations, German/regulator results, Norway and McMurdo/Black Island) with band, site, antenna, campaign and estimator context. Explain why they do not establish one global update or a single causal trend. The ITU database's geography/selection limitations describe that database, not proof that no other studies exist. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Man-made noise measured since 2000 compared with P.372”; “Contradictions…”; NTIA McMurdo subsection and Table G-1 discussion; Astra-6/50 evidence N03–N04.

5. **Track empirical values to the measured quantity.** Distinguish field strength, Fa relative to kT0, dBm/Hz, integrated receiver-band power, median/quantile, RMS, APD point, lowest-percentile floor, and occupancy fraction. A good analysis says what antenna factor, bandwidth, detector, signal excision, threshold, scan cadence and uncertainty apply before comparing values. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Units and conversions,” campaign comparison, Cyprus paragraphs and NTIA method/results; Fable-5.2/85 §§1–2 and N3–N4; Astra-6/50 evidence table.

6. **Treat amplitude statistics as bandwidth-conditioned marginals.** Explain APD/Vd and how bandwidth changes the observed envelope, while distinguishing a measured marginal from a temporal process. State when the 1985 bandwidth conversion is extrapolated below 6 Hz and MF-grounded, and the separate 1962 measured 0.64-Hz APD's limited transform validation. Neither licenses Gaussian tails or independent sub-Hz samples. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Amplitude statistics,” especially “Bandwidth conversion” and “Original narrowband evidence and its limit”; Fable-5.2/85 N2.

7. **Compare candidate stochastic models by what they capture.** Account for reference-level/decile models, component sums, APD families, Gaussian-plus-impulse/Poisson or Middleton-like processes, Bi-Kappa or alpha-stable fits as data-conditioned approximations, filtered/clustered bursts, and replay. State which observables each preserves and the evidence limits (selected records, sites, thresholds, bandwidth); avoid presenting heavy-tail fits or infinite-variance idealizations as literal universal physics. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “How the literature models or simulates this”; §§3–5; Astra-6/50 “Findings” and N08.

8. **Keep temporal, spectral and spatial structure visible.** Address burst clustering/non-Poisson arrivals, mains periodicity, lightning-flash correlation, slow power changes, carriers/sidebands and swept emissions. Cite the actual evidence domain and sample/case limits: e.g. selected one-second HF records and 10 Hz–60 kHz/VLF clustering do not set a universal HF or sub-hertz process. Include directional and spatial dependence only with their evidence limits. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Time structure,” “Interference and occupancy at HF,” model section, and additional recent evidence; Astra-6/50 N06 and band implications.

9. **Handle aggregation and dependence explicitly.** Distinguish the P.372 quantile approximation/reference implementation from a literal sum of positive random component powers; preserve the documented case where code output is below a component median and the independent-sum result differs. Do not silently fit to one interpretation and then add components again. For environmental components, shared storms, cross-band/site covariance and independence are unknown unless measured; show alternatives as sensitivity branches. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 §4 and “Contradiction matrix”; Fable-5.2/85 N1, §3 and calibration/identifiability.

10. **Name reference planes, units and conversion assumptions.** Show how field quantities, antenna-terminal power and digital I/Q relate through calibrated antenna factor/effective height or gain, polarization/pattern, mismatch, cable loss, impedance, ENBW, temperature and one-/two-sided or real/complex conventions. Keep external signal and noise together through passive loss and avoid double-counting receiver-added noise. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Units and conversions”; Fable-5.2/55 §6 “Stage order and calibrated reference planes”; Fable-5.2/85 §2 and C0.

11. **Separate congestion from waveform and link availability.** HF occupancy/congestion evidence is conditional on channel width, allocation, threshold, antenna, scan/dwell and period. It is not a PSD, waveform corpus, 250-Hz slot loss, or modem decoding probability. A scheduled-broadcaster model can inform one structured-signal layer while omitting radars, mobile/other services and broadband man-made sources. Locator: Fable-5.2/50 “Interference and occupancy at HF,” interference models, Cyprus study and SPINE evidence; Fable-5.2/85 N3 and C2.

12. **Model the receiver as an ordered, device-conditioned chain.** Explain where antenna/feed loss, preselection, LNA noise, gain, blocking/compression/intermodulation, mixer/LO phase noise and reciprocal mixing, IF filter/delay, AGC/blanker/NR, ADC clipping/quantization and I/Q calibration occur. Upstream overload cannot be undone by a later filter; a generic downstream nonlinearity is not equivalent to every real radio. Locator: Fable-5.2/55 §6, “Stage order and calibrated reference planes”; Fable-5.2/85 §2/C3.

13. **Explain when equipment effects become material.** Relate receiver NF to external-noise dominance, reciprocal mixing to blocker plus LO phase noise, and clipping/intermod/AGC capture to strong interferers. Include amplitude/group delay, time/latency, sample-clock and oscillator drift where relevant to coherent integration. Preserve device/test settings and known unmeasured limits, especially close-in phase noise and target-chain behavior. Locator: Fable-5.2/55 Physical mechanisms §§1–7; Measured and reported values A–D; contradictions; Astra-6/50 “Findings” and N11–N12.

14. **Keep band-specific hardware and sky cases in scope.** For this noise assignment, use the sub-9-kHz material only as a boundary/cross-reference; do not import its antenna or noise parameters into MF/HF. At 5.76/5.8 GHz, separate receiver/feed temperature, sky direction, Moon beam filling and local C-band RFI from MF/HF noise models. A nearby-frequency sky map, lunar brightness or occupancy survey does not automatically calibrate a target installation. Locator: Fable-5.2/55 §§8–9 and E/F; Astra-6/50 “5.8 GHz EME,” N09–N14.

15. **Translate evidence into conditional controls without claiming natural probabilities.** Sound scenarios label measured, standardized, calculated and deliberate stress values separately; preserve source-conditioned geometry, state, seed, cross-component alternatives and chunk continuity. A sample impulse, synthetic blocker or mathematical C0–C4 case is a discriminator, not an observed environment or occurrence-weighted worst case. Locator: Fable-5.2/85 §§1–4, N0–N6, C0–C4 and traceability table.

16. **Calibrate and validate with independent observables and physical holdouts.** Check field/port transfer, filtered noise power/APD, tone gain/phase/delay, blocker products, AGC trajectory/recovery, ADC overrange, timestamps/masks and state continuity before interpreting decode outcomes. Fit/validate by independent physical episode and site/device when transfer is claimed; retain censoring, missingness and uncertainty. Do not mistake a passed synthetic control or a decoder outcome for source reproduction. Locator: Fable-5.2/85 “Executed evidence checks and remaining calibration,” “Calibration, identifiability and physical holdout,” §§5–6 and “Remaining evidence gaps”; Fable-5.2/55 implementation contract; Astra-6/50 “Front-end model falsifiers.”
