## Implementation and evaluation criteria (12)

1. **Use the canonical modem end to end.** Reuse `PublicProfile`, `Emission`, `EmittedSource`, `StreamingChannel`, `CompleteStreamReceiver`, and the production message/FEC path; a contained executable may link production CMake targets, but must not copy the modem, codec, or DSP. Astra-6 §“Existing interfaces to reuse”; Fable-5.2 §“Existing integration route”. Current declarations: `include/datapump/triad_stream.hpp`, `triad_modem_v3.hpp`, `triad_stream_receiver.hpp`.

2. **Propagate the full composite waveform.** Compose DATA carriers, Helper, silence and source calendar before one channel realization; do not split carrier identity into independent channels. Preserve canonical source ordinal and object lifetime. The appraisal's `src/main.cpp` native probe path (around `inputs`/`composite`) demonstrates summing complete emissions and sending one continuous source through `StreamingChannel`; current `EmittedSource` contract is `include/datapump/triad_modem_v3.hpp:122` onward. The research diagrams also place TX/antenna, channel, noise/interferers and RX/front end in physical order.

3. **Separate RF frequency from sampled bandwidth.** A 5.8 GHz carrier can be represented by a complex envelope at occupied-band sample rates; conversely, narrow DATA bandwidth does not bound a distant Helper/blocker/front-end mechanism. Model whole-waveform band edges and rate/aliasing explicitly. Astra-6 §“What ‘triple’ means here” and §“A proposed processing boundary”.

4. **Select model complexity by observable and evidence.** Alternatives include recorded IQ, measured response replay, conditioned stochastic FIR/taps, geometry plus stochastic residual, detailed solver, and validated-domain surrogate. State the cost/misspecification and discriminating holdout for each. A named package or a physically detailed model is neither integration nor validation. Astra-6 §“Compare several implementation families”; Fable-5.2 §“Candidate channel representations”.

5. **Require conditioned physical inputs and explicit reference planes.** Frequency alone cannot pick conductivity, terrain, season, ionospheric path or EME geometry. Name station/path/environment, polarization, antenna and receiver planes; apply antenna efficiency, matching/feed loss, external noise and receiver noise exactly once. Fable-5.2 §“State and signal conventions”; `research-reconciliation.md` rows for VLF, 1 MHz, 30 MHz, HF and EME.

6. **Keep coupled observables and nonlinear stage order.** One operator on the complete emission should create intended cross-carrier/Helper correlation. Place propagation, additive sources, filtering, AGC/nonlinearity and conversion in calibrated physical order. A sample process that independently fits each carrier or a blocker that bypasses overload cannot answer the common-channel/front-end question. Astra-6 §“A proposed processing boundary”; Fable-5.2 §“State and signal conventions”; appraisal `tests/test_impairment_observables.cpp` and `test_channel.cpp` inventory.

7. **Prove chunk/state invariance.** Changing read chunk or scheduling must not reset clocks, modes, noise, source ordinal, filter state or delayed energy. Use index-stable random streams when needed; keep physically shared correlations intact. Appraisal `tests/test_integration.py:341–344,470–472` checks waveform digests and HF history for a 1021-sample chunk; this is finite coverage, not all models. Production channel state/tail code is `src/triad_simulation_stream.cpp` (state construction around 515–743; bounded reads around 1017 onward).

8. **Bound delay and filter history from declared support.** Retain variable/fractional-delay interpolation and channel/front-end tails, or give a residual-energy bound for truncated infinite-memory models. Do not treat the implementation history cap as a scientific maximum; do not store seconds of zeros for bulk delay when timestamps suffice. Source locator: `src/triad_simulation_stream.cpp:515–574`; Astra-6 §“A proposed processing boundary”.

9. **Keep hot processing native and account for work.** Channel DSP and persistent receiver state belong in integrated C++ (Python may orchestrate/analyze). Time source/channel generation separately from receiver and end-to-end execution. Parallel episode throughput is not single-stream capacity; preserve full public receiver hypotheses and Helper/search/tracking/reacquisition/quiet costs. Astra-6 §“Resource and implementation limits”; `docs/development.md` receiver allocation contract referenced by both research chapters.

10. **Admit bounded memory and computation before execution.** Account channel buffers, latent state, receiver live-owner/DSP/input/stack/results and retained evidence; include precomputation. Reject unadmitted work instead of silently omitting endpoints. Fable-5.2 §“Resources and validation” gives examples of extreme EME cell-sample and VLF physical-time cost, explicitly not timing estimates. The appraisal has plan/resource boundaries in `tests/test_integration.py` and per-run receipts described in `README.md` §“Native modes and bounds”.

11. **Keep public receiver inputs separate from private truth.** Current scope is configured calendar, epoch, ordinal/key, waveform and FEC; unknown-start/ordinal acquisition requires competing wrong-address hypotheses and its full cost. Evaluator truth may score outputs but cannot reveal bits, termination or realized fade. Preserve physical completion: two scored quiet slots; erasures are not noise-only samples, and EOF/cancel is not silence. Astra-6 §“Receiver implications”; current `include/datapump/triad_stream_receiver.hpp:92–107` and `src/triad_message_receiver.cpp` quiet-run path.

12. **Separate evidence gates.** (a) implementation/math checks (identity, units, signs, tails, covariance, chunking, initialization, nonlinear recovery, resource rejection); (b) source-observable reproduction under the source estimator/selection/censoring; (c) target-domain empirical validation; (d) frozen complete-message receiver behavior; (e) same-stream hardware capacity. Passing one does not imply another. Numerical convergence is not natural-model evidence; a complete build is not physical fidelity; CPU/GPU fallback is not device performance. Fable-5.2 §“Resources and validation” and `80-completion-plan.md` §“Later implementation and qualification sequence”.
