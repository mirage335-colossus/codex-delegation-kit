## What counts as full depth

Depth is functional and evidential, not a word count or a number-of-papers contest. A topic is addressed at equivalent depth when the treatment makes its decision-relevant alternatives and assumptions explicit, supports consequential claims with inspectable evidence or reproducible calculations, tests at least one plausible failure mode/counterexample, and states what the evidence cannot establish. A heading, citation list, equation without inputs, or confident recommendation without a scope boundary is only topic mention.

Use a three-level judgment per criterion: **0 — absent or materially misleading; 1 — named but incomplete (missing a decision-changing alternative, evidence, counterexample or limit); 2 — substantively supported and bounded.** This is a diagnostic rubric, not an aggregate score or acceptance threshold. Mark “not applicable” only with a scope rationale. A polished synthesis may satisfy a criterion across several linked sections; it need not repeat every argument in every chapter. Preserve contradictions and case provenance when comparing claims.

## Evaluation criteria

1. **Question, estimand and applicability.** The writer distinguishes the research question, receiver decision and physical quantity being estimated; identifies frequency, geometry, antenna/reference plane, time scale and population; and says which claims are about a test model versus nature. Check 00 §§1, 3.1–3.4 and 4–6; Astra-6 “What this review can establish” and “Five different meanings of adverse conditions.”

2. **Alternative simulation/calibration routes.** Compare genuinely different options—analytic/reference models, standards, calibrated stochastic episodes, physics-driven models and replay—by what each reproduces and omits. Where implementation choices matter, state consequences of filtered noise, FFT blocks, sinusoid sums, autoregression, replay, independent taps and coupled scatterers; do not declare one universally best because it is detailed or fast. Check 00 §6; 60 “Why each generator family has an intrinsic error,” §§A, D, E, “How the literature models or simulates this,” and “What a high-fidelity simulation would need.”

3. **Physical law to sampled operation.** Derive or justify the channel operation from geometry/physical state to phase, delay, Doppler, frequency-dependent response, polarization, noise and sampled implementation. Avoid double-counting carrier Doppler and delay motion, circular wrap, false compact support, wrong reference planes or arbitrary power renormalization. Check 00 §§3.1–3.3, 5 and 7; 60 “From a physical path to a sampled operation,” “Time-varying delay,” and Astra-6 “A physical description before a fading generator.”

4. **Calibration versus validation and discrepancy.** Separate parameter fitting, numerical verification, reproduction of an instrument's observable, independent holdouts, end-to-end receiver outcomes and hardware/capacity claims. Include model discrepancy and instrumentation uncertainty; state why mean-power matching alone cannot validate outages, bursts or coherent tracking. Check Astra-6 “Fidelity is validated at several levels”; 60 validation observables and finite-process contract; 86 “Executed finite-method research checks.”

5. **Dependence, nonstationarity and cross-component structure.** State what is shared or independent across paths, carriers, time windows and equipment; distinguish a dependence model from reusing seeds. Treat outage/resumption, transitions, cross-frequency relation, delay-Doppler coupling and persistent states explicitly. Check 00 §7 “Randomness and numerical decisions”; 60 §§7, 9 and “Nonstationarity and shared paths”; 86 finite-process and C3/state checks; 87 S37–S43.

6. **Extremes and “worst case.”** Distinguish observed severe event, conditional population tail, physically compatible stress, adversarial stress and outage/unavailability. Do not multiply marginal maxima into an asserted joint event or call a scenario a percentile without a population and exposure basis. Discuss dependence/tail uncertainty and what is merely stress coverage. Check 00 §3.4; Astra-6 “Five different meanings of adverse conditions” and “Rare failures and bounded computation”; 87 C07–C08 and S49–S55.

7. **Definitions and observable provenance.** For every consequential numeric result, carry units and conventions: signal/noise plane, PSD sidedness and ENBW, averaging, delay/Doppler width definition, estimator, window, sounder resolution/threshold, censoring, exposure, and whether a value is measured, standardized, derived, proposed or extrapolated. Check 00 §§2–3; Astra-6 “Definitions that must travel with every number”; 60 source-estimator guidance; 90 §4 and §6.

8. **Statistical unit and estimand.** Name the independent unit and target population; keep episode-level outcomes together where bits/slots/samples are dependent. Separate fixed diagnostic roots, paired contrasts, conditional success probability, environmental prevalence, emulator accuracy, and complete-receiver capacity. Check 60 §§9, G and selected statistical design; 86 “Selected inferential purpose and procedure,” finite experiment inventory and endpoint.

9. **Finite-sample inference, multiplicity and looks.** Give a valid interval/test for the declared sample unit, account for predeclared endpoints/strata, state stopping and censoring rules, and explain sequential alternatives' assumptions. Demonstrate why an attractive rule can fail (e.g. zero-failure intervals, the rejected adjusted 20/20 plan, or repeated looks). Check 60 §G and “Selected statistical design”; 86 “Fixed conditional confirmation,” “Exact interval reconstruction,” “Admission budget”; 87 S49, S51, S53–S56.

10. **Rare-event methods and weighting.** If importance sampling, subset simulation or tail extrapolation is proposed, state the target law, proposal support, likelihood ratios, weight diagnostics and validation. If those inputs are missing, do not report an outage probability from deliberately adverse cases. Include counterexamples to naive proposals where relevant. Check Astra-6 “Rare failures and bounded computation”; 60 §G and its limits; 86 S55; 87 S55.

11. **Matched comparisons and false confidence.** Freeze equal inputs, endpoints, receiver hypotheses, environment and seeds where pairing is intended; define what is matched versus merely nominally equal. Prevent truth leakage, per-record normalization, repeated timing masquerading as independent replications, hidden exclusions, success-only averages and component timings standing in for receiver performance. Check 00 §7; Astra-6 “Fidelity is validated at several levels” and “Rare failures”; 86 pilot/confirmation, objective invalidity and capacity-admission rules; 87 S41, S49–S57.

12. **Arithmetic and independent calculation quality.** Show equations, constants, units, exact input cases, independent method/toolchain, rounding/convergence criteria and a checkable result. Explain what agreement verifies and what it does not. Include failed checks and corrections, not only selected passing examples. Check 86 “Scalar arithmetic checks,” “Expanded arithmetic coverage,” “Executed finite-method research checks”; 90 §§1.1–7, especially the NTIA LFMF identity, full-vector residuals and §7 limitations.

13. **Counterexamples and contrary evidence.** Test whether a rule generalizes beyond its assumptions: e.g. Gaussian versus Lorentzian equal-correlation shapes have different fade crossings and power variance; two-ray frequency correlation can rebound at a second spacing; applying a bandwidth conversion below its measurement band can contradict the assumed limiting distribution; per-record normalization hides fade variance; finite sinusoid methods have construction-specific ergodicity behavior. Check 60 §§A–G and “Contradictions”; 86 “Width, correlation and coherent integration,” “Shape, finite-record normalization and state,” and S35–S45; 90 §6.

14. **Primary-source quality and recency.** Trace important standards and claims to the relevant original/official text, version and clause; distinguish publication, observation and indexing dates; mark full-text, abstract-only, secondary, code-only or unavailable access; and explain transfer limits of adjacent domains. Search for recent primary work where it can change the method, while preserving contrary and inaccessible leads as such. Check 00 §2; 60 §H, dated gap dispositions, “Follow-through source receipts” and contextual-reference dispositions; 87 C03 and G15; Astra-6 “Search coverage and selection.”

15. **Traceability and appraisal uptake.** Link each decision-driving claim, calculation and unresolved question to a stable locator/source identity, a scenario or appraisal consumer, and a disposition. Keep evidence, proposed method, executed check, failed check and external/native follow-up distinguishable. Reopen conclusions when a newer source or corrected input changes them. Check 00 §8; 60 dated source dispositions; 87 “Cross-cutting obligations,” G15 and S35–S59; 90 opening and section-specific applicability notes.

16. **Feasibility and explicit unrun boundary.** Bound work by the actual geometry, integer sample count, memory, runtime, complete state/tails and receiver workload. Do not replace expensive valid work by an unqualified shortcut, nor invoke huge campaigns when a small mathematical discriminator answers the narrow question. Mark unadmitted/unrun checks as such and identify the exact missing evidence. Check 60 selected finite-process and statistical design; 86 “Admission budget before any whole-message campaign”; Astra-6 “Rare failures and bounded computation”; 87 S38–S43 and S52–S59.

## Corpus-specific caveats for evaluators

- The corpus itself expressly labels the 00/60/86/90 records as research, source inspection or bounded mathematical checks, not modem experiments or environmental validation. Equivalent-depth evaluation must preserve that separation.
- The audit's 200 stable obligations are a consequential-claim audit denominator, not 200 passing tests or proof of exhaustive coverage. The original roughly 234,000-word draft size and the present inventory word counts are not quality thresholds.
- The strongest unresolved boundaries named in these sources include natural long-duration channel calibration, target-domain complex measurement records and joint distributions, actual native generator/moving-delay/chunk-invariance checks, whole-message receiver trials, receiver sensitivity to selected numerical approximations, and hardware/capacity qualification. They should remain open unless neighboring evidence directly closes them.
- The review of six files does not validate every scenario's source attribution, every independent equation, every appraisal run or the entire underlying literature. It provides a map for that review and grounded alternatives for recognizing the same depth elsewhere.
