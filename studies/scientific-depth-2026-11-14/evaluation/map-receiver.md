## Overall assessment

The corpus is materially deep and unusually self-correcting. It combines standards and historical validation, contradictory propagation interpretations, measured-record access audits, model/receiver contracts, causal diagnostics, explicit negative results, and resource accounting. It does not collapse model plausibility, numerical implementation, receiver success, or real-world prevalence into one claim.

The most important unresolved scientific items are joint measured phase/amplitude/delay/mode statistics over long disturbed intervals; wider-band and multi-population validation of model families; a calibrated nonstationary trajectory law; the benefit uniquely attributable to three carriers; reliability outside the small constructed pilot cohorts; and complete-service qualification on intended hardware. These are accurately presented as open research/qualification questions rather than failures to search an already known answer. The newer appraisal has significantly advanced implementation and causal evidence since both resilience reports, but its run claims remain source-specific and should not be retroactively attributed to those reports.
