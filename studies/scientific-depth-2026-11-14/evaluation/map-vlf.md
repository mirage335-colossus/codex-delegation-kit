## How to use the criteria

Score each criterion 0 (missing or materially misleading), 1 (partial, one-sided, or weakly bounded), or 2 (substantive, supported and applied to the stated case). Give a concrete locator in the new work and a concise reason. A valid alternative scientific approach can satisfy a criterion; do not require the same cited papers, word count, or selected model. A bibliography, mention of “VLF,” or a technically named algorithm alone is not substantive coverage. Where criteria touch adjacent subjects, credit the actual distinction: propagation is not antenna efficiency; an antenna input is not calibrated receiver C/N0; noise PSD is not impulsiveness or post-processing statistics; a runnable scenario is not empirical qualification.

For each evidence claim, look for the source identity and the actual section/table/figure; what was measured versus calculated, standardized, modeled, extrapolated, or reported by practitioners; applicable frequency, path, site, instrument, bandwidth and uncertainty; material counterevidence; and the resulting modeling or implementation choice. Alternative sources can replace exemplars if they support the same scientific distinction. Do not reward citations whose inspected content does not support the claim.

## Corpus-open versus new-work-absent

These labels must not be conflated in scoring or synthesis:

* **Open in the reference corpus** means the reference itself states that a scientific or empirical question remains unresolved, unmeasured, inaccessible, or model-dependent. Examples include sub-4-kHz attenuation disagreement and lack of coherent carrier statistics (Fable-5.2/10, “Contradictions, open questions, and things not found”); lack of matched sub-9-kHz antenna efficiency / full trial denominators and calibrated receiver chain (Fable-5.2/11, “Contradictions, open questions, and things not found”); noise-tail and marked-event gaps (Fable-5.2/12, “Contradictions, open questions, and things not found”); and the empirical dependencies enumerated in Fable-5.2/81 §7–8.
* **Absent from the prior narrow study** means no corresponding VLF/sub-9 treatment was located in the listed earlier participant results, uptake report, or frozen HF/EME rubric materials. This observation must not be used to score fresh depth-campaign results; it says nothing about whether the reference corpus or external literature contains the topic.
* An answer that says “unknown” should identify which kind of unknown it means, how it searched/read, what evidence was unavailable, and what observation would change the decision. Do not award substantive coverage for silently omitting the VLF domains because the previous exploratory rubric held out HF and EME.
