The first run writes the full sets of norms, shape/window, pointing, field covariance, reduction error, sky/polarization case and memory/operation outputs; the second repeats them at the prior 720,000-point resolution. Matched 28 model/dish/Doppler rows moved by at most 0.00555 ms in delay quantiles, 0.113 Hz in the outer frequency quantiles (orthogonal stress), and 0.029 percentage points in the reported window powers. The severe captured result changed by 0.002 dB. Fitted early fraction, exact retarded function, conditional gradients, old beam and no-weather uniform observations were checked against the prior artifacts. Analytical checks include normalized mass, `2/3` D2 bare integral and both Gaussian disk integrals. An early 250×90 setup stopped at its first overstrict normalization assertion (zero checks passed in that attempt); the repaired quadrature-scaled check passed the coarse run and both full desk grids. The first draft convergence check expected 0.06 Hz frequency quantile stability and failed at 0.113 Hz; retain actual discretization, report the quantile comparison to Hz-scale precision and do not treat that desk expectation as product acceptance. In all these checks, “pass” means mathematical consistency, not accuracy against the actual Moon or receiver.

Field covariance and long-lag speckle are more sensitive to quadrature aliasing than rectangle counts. At the mixture's 1 s lag, quadrature |ρ| moves from .0031 to .0015; near-zero values at long lags are not timing/independence thresholds. The phase-referenced 5 ms, 20 ms and joint covariance, and the static-centroid errors there, repeat to the displayed three decimals for this mixture. There is no guaranteed measured coherence time, SNR, acquisition result, service rate or weather occurrence.
