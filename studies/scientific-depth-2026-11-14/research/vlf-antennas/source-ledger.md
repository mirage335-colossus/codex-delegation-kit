The 61m top-loaded vertical calculation is reproduced by [`wire_case_checks.py`](wire_case_checks.py) and its stdout by [`wire_case_checks.txt`](wire_case_checks.txt). It uses the NPL bare-wire capacitance formula, the top-loaded short-monopole radiation-resistance relationship from OT Report77-131, and explicitly hypothetical effective-height/top-hat-capacitance, coil-Q, conductor/earth-loss and receiver-noise values. It converts base current to ideal free-space/ground-plane E/B, loop-terminal voltage and conditional C/N0, while also quantifying resonator bandwidth, detuning, high voltage and settling. No physical antenna, soil, field site, or modem link was measured.
