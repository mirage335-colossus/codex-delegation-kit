# HF regimes branch

- `chapter.md`: decision-ready reusable scientific synthesis and project mapping.
- `source-ledger.md`: exact sources, locators, provenance, applicability and access gaps.
- `hf_calcs.py` / `hf_calcs.txt`: reproducible analytical desk check and actual stdout.
- `hf_observation_check.py` / `.txt`: bounded arithmetic on Dear et al.'s published NVIS relative-CIR observation and the availability-denominator limit.
- `gillies_absorption_check.py` / `.txt`: arithmetic and hypothetical operation-denominator sensitivity based on Gillies et al. (2026) published aggregate summaries; no raw pulse counts/logs are reconstructed.
- `tep_geometry_check.py` / `.txt`: spherical distance/bearing check from Maruyama & Kawamura's published TEP transmitter/receiver coordinates; no ionospheric ray or channel is inferred.
- `watterson_reconstruction.py` / `.txt`: independent reconstruction from three transcribed Watterson (1969) aggregate-mode tables: linear normalized tap powers and mixture delay/Doppler mean and 2σ widths; magnetoionic component rows excluded to avoid double counting.
- `chapter-first-pass.md` / `source-ledger-first-pass.md`: preserved original branch draft before review-directed corrections.
