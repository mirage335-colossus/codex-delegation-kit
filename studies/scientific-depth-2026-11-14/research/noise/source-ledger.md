## Reproducible desk calculation

Script `noise_sensitivity.py`, inputs/formulas documented in its header/comments and chapter. Run from kit root:

```text
python3 studies/scientific-depth-2026-11-14/research/noise/noise_sensitivity.py
```

Observed output is preserved in `noise_sensitivity.out`. No external dataset was downloaded. Assumptions are 290 K, P.372-17 median (F_a) curves, 3 kHz equivalent rectangular noise bandwidth, lossless reference antenna, unshielded galactic value, uniform lunar disk of 0.5° diameter and Gaussian main beam with approximate (58\lambda/D) HPBW. This is a conditional sensitivity calculation, not an assertion about installed hardware or current P.372-18.
