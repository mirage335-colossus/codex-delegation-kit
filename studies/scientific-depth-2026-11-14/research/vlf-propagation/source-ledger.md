## Search scope and exclusions

Search batches queried (a) ITU/NBS classical VLF mode sources, (b) low-band ELF/VLF waveguide measurements and spectra, (c) dawn/dusk and solar flare/space-weather disturbance observations, (d) a focused search for <10 kHz transmitter measurements, field-strength reports, mode polarization and ground conductivity, and (e) high-latitude particle precipitation and storm statistics. First-party ITU, NIST/GovInfo and primary AGU/Radio Science/IEEE papers were prioritized. Searches also returned older model/code references (LWPC/ModeFinder) and abstracts, but no project code or unsupplied evaluations were accessed. Wikipedia/community snippets were not used as evidence. Recent articles were checked for publication state and their limits are stated. A preprint surfaced in search but was not relied on.

## Measured evidence versus calculation and inference

* S3 and S4/S7/S8/S9 are observational sources, but different observables: satellite cutoff maps, beacon monitor phase/amplitude, lightning-network paired peaks, or one disturbance-event time series. They should not be pooled into one distribution.
* S2 contains both modeled outputs and a literature-reported measured lightning spectrum/model comparison; its mode/FDTD run is not a measured modem link.
* S5–S6 support conditional path/mode/surface effects but include fitted or synthetic parts and regime limits; numerical results are not universal environmental bounds.
* `regime_scales.py` is a deterministic dimensional calculation, not an observation or product check.
* “Use complex (H(f)) as the boundary” and the proposed validation sequence are engineering recommendations/inferences from the multi-mode equations and observation limits. Their success remains to be tested with project-matched carriers, antennas, paths and receiver capacity.
