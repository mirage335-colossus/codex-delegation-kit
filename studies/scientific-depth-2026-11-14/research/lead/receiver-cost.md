## The extra computation

Use a frozen first-order proper-complex Gaussian innovation law from independent, calibrated noise-only training and its matched identity control. Use the same raw-plane candidate templates, known `a` real with `|a|<1`, and a contiguous fully valid window. With noise `x[n] = a x[n−1] + e[n]` and innovation variance `σ_e² = E|e|²`, the stationary finite-window whitener is `(Wx)[0] = sqrt(1−a²) x[0]`, `(Wx)[n] = x[n]−a x[n−1]`. The first row assumes the stationary marginal variate. No state or information is carried over an unobserved hole in this branch. Generate both native DATA patterns from the known key and public format adjusted for the selected epoch and ordinal. Render at the selected arrival/clock in the actual public sample coordinate and apply the corresponding carrier rotation and conversion **before** this operator. Existing bounded `PatternSource` is the reusable pulse/keyed-waveform primitive. Check exact mapping and energy against the admitted source; the coarse or fine receiver's projected basis is not assumed to yield an interchangeable raw template. Whitening, phase mixing and decimation need not commute. For first implementation and conservative counting, visit all `N = stop_sample−first_sample` rows, including any zero-padding/support edge where finite filtering extends a template by one row.

At one coordinate and bit let `y` be the full raw observation, `s` its raw template, and `g ~ CN(0,P_g)` the single gain prior. Compute `q=(Ws)ᴴWy`, `G=||Ws||²`, and use the normalized scalar likelihood ratio versus the same colored noise model for both bits:

```text
log Λ = −log1p(P_g G / σ_e²)
        + P_g |q|² / [σ_e² (σ_e² + P_g G)]
```

Use the identity `a=0` control on the same templates and window. If the two diagnostics are calibrated under a common physical held-out null, compare predictive score/order and observed detections at separately matched false-event rates. An identity working model at a fitted marginal variance is a mismatch control for colored data. Its raw evidence does not share the white model's finite-sample guarantee in that case. The shadow makes no affirmative absence decision.

The Helper-on fixture can price the complete shadow workload on its original raw input. Interpreting evidence as a covariance-only science result requires either a declared Helper-off nuisance-free control from the matched pilot or a qualified shared Helper/line residual or parametric nuisance basis. A deterministic tone or an unmodeled Helper contribution violates the noise-only model and can make both scores misleading. Label affected raw scores unqualified; do not fit away only the observation or use a narrow or partial aperture to certify silence. A future shared nuisance transform must act on the data, both bit templates and the null law. Its projection cost, covariance/rank and stopping rule belong in a new measured quote. A single scalar gain also deliberately limits this test to the noise-weighting question; it does not exercise the chapter's distinct multi-path covariance alternative. Use stationary-colored known-signal and blank controls for an isolated weighting result, with a separate stressed v. valid-null test in the real fixture.

## Pilot and decision

Use the five-regime 20-record validation pilot to screen calibration mistakes and compare A/B on the **same captured records**. The 20 records give 40 paired-arm replay runs and 620 active-source seconds per arm. Before preflight the total input duration and bytes remain parametric. For each record starting at sample zero, compute the natural length from prepared lead, timing-adjusted source including ramps/calendar, capture trail, and the physical, selective, interpolation and frontend tails. The live non-noise simulation sets `minimum_capture_samples = calendar.slot(end_slot).observation.stop`, an absolute stop that already reserves two quiet slots, and the channel emits `N = max(N_natural, minimum_capture_samples)`. For a declared later capture start use the extent on the same sample axis. Retain a longer observed tail when applicable and check the two declared quiet apertures separately in preflight. If a physical tail contaminates them, recompute the public fixture before use or withhold valid quiet. Allocating extra samples does not repair a contaminated aperture. Sum each record's own sample count divided by its prepared RX sample rate for input duration, and 17 times its sample count per IQ+mask lane for bytes. The HF fixture's 6 ksample/s cannot set other regimes' rates. The initial 652 s per arm estimate used arbitrary tails and undercounted required input. It remains recorded in the desk output and validation follow-up as superseded. For this shadow, fix noise-model training to independent blank data and keep target/holdout data separate. Include Helper or strong line, false-quiet/erasure, stationary colored known-signal/known-blank, and a covariance model mismatch (e.g. impulsive bursts) under explicit strata. Capture the complete service time and stage counters separately in every replay; the recorded seconds and the desk operation counts do not establish `<1 s/s`. If the extra scoring is not helpful at matched false-event risk on eligible held-out controls, retain the ordinary likelihood and investigate analog preselection, noise calibration or candidate coverage using their separate controls. If helpful, proceed first to bounded calibrated nuisance modelling, then to a common-null/operational test and conformance/capacity on the complete hard workloads.
