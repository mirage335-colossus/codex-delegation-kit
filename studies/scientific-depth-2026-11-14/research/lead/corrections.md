# Consequential reconciliations from synthesis review

These are corrections to lead claims or to inherited early notes used in the synthesis; branch originals and direct measurement outputs are retained in the branch paths. They are not new source claims.

GUI bounded two-ray cannot combine with outage/moving delay/diurnal; additional conditional per-mode/multi-event support should have evidence. Two equal stationary Gaussian paths already available and tested at helper scope; low-width quiet uses fixed physical clock rather than introducing another generator or moving the rate. |

| “35/90 Hz echo widths” initially read as measured; a first correction then omitted the observed spectrum extents | [7.209 GHz paper §§2–3 and Figs. 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7180880/) | Predicted beam-conditioned geometric spans are 35/90 Hz from DE436 point velocities. Neither pair supplies a measured RMS width, FWHM or precisely measured support. The −15/−10 Hz center residuals and roughly 7 Hz Km session standard deviation describe other observables. Preserve radiometer-style SNR vs Pr/kTB. |
| The deliberately orthogonal kinematic stress could be mistaken for a lunar Doppler law. The 1,000/2,000 radial-cell comparisons supersede the coarse core estimate. Ordinary CW does not measure joint excess delay and Doppler. |

The moment proxy improves covariance for one mixture. It has larger errors on a diffuse case and supplies no validated waveform rule. Calibrated replay remains the fade-sensitive reference. |
| Wet signal loss, noise heating, polarization and capture effects could be pooled into one weather margin. | [Separate endpoint calculation](../eme-atmosphere/chapter.md) · [Joined visibility and capture case](../eme-integration/chapter.md#a-compatible-severe-visible-endpoint-case) | Keep a TX and RX signal loss, one receive brightness transform, explicit equipment and sky conditions, and ideal windowed power separate. The conditional clear/wet difference cannot be assigned to rain alone or used as a probability. A physically blocked Moon may leave valid noise samples on the receiver. |

Nominal 16s group shear alone may be negligible; longer/open clock cases or actual IF conditions determine whether to use a new resampler. |
