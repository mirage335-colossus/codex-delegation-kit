## Calculation provenance

[`endpoint_case.py`](endpoint_case.py) uses only Python's standard library and the listed equation coefficients. It is import-safe; `compute_case(elevation_deg, wet)` returns keys `gas_db`, `cloud_db`, `rain_db`, `eta`, `emit_K`, `tx_leg_loss_db`, `rx_leg_loss_db`, and signal reference/result powers. The captured execution output is [`endpoint_case.out`](endpoint_case.out). No outside package, installed library or pump source is used.

The atmosphere geometry is same-route reciprocal TX/RX at the same apparent elevation. The 10° rain/cloud conditions are intentionally co-located across one finite 1 km vertical slab, with the cloud-liquid and rain-specific attenuations added as distinct hydrometeor terms; the slab is broad enough to cover a 5.67 km horizontal path. Both signal legs receive a separate copy of the one-way attenuation. Receiver sky/Moon brightness passes the RX layer once, using `T_out=ηT_in+(1−η)T_layer`; antenna spillover to ground and receiver noise bypass this sky-layer transform. No universal bound, weather co-occurrence rate, or ensemble claim is made.
