# Frozen final workflow evaluation

This extends the original protocol after development exposed failures in v2 and
v3. No h/f fresh-case model turn has run. Their prepare-only records remain.
The final g-series preserves the predeclared four cases, two repetitions per
condition and randomized order. The candidate is the final source/workflow
snapshot v4; the baseline remains the maintained draft at this request's start.

Initial owners receive identical task/input information, command/write authority,
Luna/high/default settings, and a 240-second owned runtime limit. Initialization
is serialized and at most two model turns overlap, identically for both arms.
Case outcomes, commands, patch/artifacts, protected files, reporting and ordinary
checks are independently assessed. Keep raw private-oracle observations separate
from the actual task contract. The predeclared empty runtime-lock normalization
is recorded independently of raw scope results; no other outputs are exempted.

The candidate workflow combines the actual backend instructions with the native
coordinator procedure exercised during development. The controller's audited
contradiction disposition is separately verified by integration tests; these model
runs do not pretend to be full persistent-controller executions.

Both arms receive their ordinary authorized independent review and bounded
repair/strong-owner path; a failed baseline is not artificially truncated.
When a candidate has an actionable substantive failure, the coordinator follows
existing repair/strong-owner rules, retains the first result, and uses independent
original-contract reconstruction if the rule or framing remains at issue. That
branch receives stable original source, task, constraints and raw observations in
a fresh context; its initial contract/checks are retained before current candidate
exposure. It then reconciles against the candidate and executes any consequential
discriminator. Current implementation review may run concurrently. Strong
engineering ownership arbitrates requirements versus helper assumptions and
settles the correction. Bounded implementation and independent acceptance follow.
No user steering or task-specific answer is supplied to the original-contract
branch. Additional study stages have explicitly recorded limits and ancestry;
they do not erase consumed production allowances or pretend to be first attempts.

A passing control receives no unnecessary repair or challenge round. A genuine
permission/resource boundary remains binding. If a baseline requires recovery,
use its frozen baseline guidance and ordinary authorized path; label any later
application of the new workflow separately. Report initial versus after-recovery
results and effort.

All initial requests are frozen before any result is opened. For any later
per-run repair or engineering decision, supply only that run's original task,
source and observed evidence to fresh engineering context. The root relays the
frozen procedure and run-local evidence; it must not pass discoveries or
counterexamples from another repetition, or supply a case-specific solution.
Retain the exact later packet to audit this boundary. Analysis and grading may
compare repetitions after their outputs are sealed, but that comparison cannot
steer unfinished participants. Report any deviation rather than claiming
independent repetitions.

The development c/z/x sequence demonstrates a selected, coordinated recovery
process. These fresh cases assess transfer and control behavior. If they require
no additional recovery branch, they cannot establish new independent evidence of
that branch's activation or reliability. Neither this small suite nor policy text
establishes a universal autonomous correction guarantee or isolates parallel
scheduling speed from extra independent reasoning effort.
