The scheduling design is therefore coupled: more owners alone still divides the same CPU budget, and admission is also constrained by backlog, input reservations, and workspace. The c54 predeclared cap is 3.75 GiB; its control combined estimate is 4,018,893,741 B, about 99.81% of cap (modeled workspace, not an OS hard cap). The ABBA memory checks passed at a different frozen 5 GiB cap, but neither result establishes feasibility for another workload.
- The source dependency chain is public planning/bank preparation -> owner/native ingress and coarse candidates -> persistent tracking across segments -> ordered parent decision/message completion. `triad_tracking_basis.py` submits row ranges to an executor and joins the futures before releasing tile/output storage. This bounds tracking parallelism and its memory lifetime. The traced c54 DATA/QUIET slots attribute roughly 83–90% of slot service to tracking, with 136–167 million basis rows per segment; coarse FFT/score work is much smaller. That makes tracking/basis the strongest next profiling target, but the evidence does not show whether those executor workers were CPU-running or idle. Must collect worker/OS CPU telemetry before diagnosing task supply or claiming utilization.

## Requirements, choices, and limitations

- **Case-specific hard outcomes from the historical request:** complete CPU forecasting of the manually specified suite, meaningful measurement on the computer's 12 hardware threads, credible CPU margin evidence, and a fast GPU-assisted forecasting method. They are not satisfied by the isolated ABBA/trace.
- **Predeclared experiment gates (hard for that experiment):** 15% median resident service gain, both matched resident ratios below 1, no more than 5% cold lifecycle regression, plus parity, provenance, memory, and runtime gates. Resident gain failed; cold and paired ratio conditions passed. Preserve `performance_gate_rejected`.
- **Adjustable design choices:** default owner count, worker split, GPU precision/method, and which bounded stage to optimize. The evidence supports retaining two owners for this measured case and examining tracking only if current-source profiling demonstrates a schedulable imbalance. It does not establish an unconditional need to change the default worker count.
- **Limits:** c54 is an earlier checkpoint than the ABBA source revision; no evidence equates their source identities. The trace is a five-window configured-calendar row-7 stream and an early fine segment, not a broad sensitivity qualification. ABBA is one configured-calendar row-7 stream with a short screen and one qualification; it is not suite coverage or sustained CPU stress. Worker interval spans, thread CPU, process CPU and OS utilization are distinct measures. Equal outputs alone would not prove matched inputs; here the receipt audit verifies the declared input hashes for ABBA, and the trace hashes match before/after within c54.
