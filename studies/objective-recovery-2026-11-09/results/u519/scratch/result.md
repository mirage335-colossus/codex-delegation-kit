## Decision

The proposed rejection of the three-owner, 18-worker route (`p3w18`) should stand. Its one-look seven-window screen selected it for a predeclared independent qualifier; the screen was a selector, not proof of adoption. The four-look 13-window ABBA completed with full receiver-work coverage and matching exposed work/message results. The paired resident service medians were 18.613 s (`p2w12`) and 17.889 s (`p3w18`), a 3.92% reduction. The gate required at least 15%, and both paired ratios below 1; the ratios (0.9455, 0.9772) satisfy the latter but not the decisive magnitude gate. Complete-lifecycle median improved 6.69%, within the allowed 5% regression bound. Recomputed average busy-core equivalents rose from 4.12 to 5.78, but utilization alone does not satisfy the speed objective. The independent receipt audit verified hashes/inputs while running, full selected-span union/envelope/gap arithmetic, parity, memory checks and CPU accounting; zero fallback/retry. The performance gate itself was intentionally rejected, so this evidence supports retaining the prior two-owner/default behavior and not adopting the wider route.
