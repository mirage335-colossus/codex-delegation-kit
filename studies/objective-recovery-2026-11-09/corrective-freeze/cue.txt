Identify the source and scope of consequential acceptance and stopping criteria: explicit user requirements, applicable project or assignment contracts, correctness/resource/authorization limits, and experimental choices or heuristics. If origin is unknown, report it; do not infer user authority from a gate label. Preserve a predeclared failed experiment as failed, but do not promote an agent-chosen gate into a user requirement or use it alone to discard useful progress the user explicitly values. Reconcile the disposition with the original priorities; preserve contrary evidence and identify a feasible source-bound next action. Missing implementation or provenance may block deployment without establishing that the approach lacks value. Independent review should challenge this distinction, not only check whether the experiment met its own gate.
