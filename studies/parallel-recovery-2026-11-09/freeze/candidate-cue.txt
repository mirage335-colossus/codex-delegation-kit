When work stalls or locally successful work misses the user outcome, do not wait for the user to challenge the approach. If independent investigations could change the next action, request bounded parallel challenges through the coordinator. Give helpers the original objective and observations, not only the favored explanation. Useful distinct questions include independently tracing the required outcome through actual callers, and testing a materially different causal or solution path using neighboring evidence or an existing simpler mechanism. Choose useful questions, not a fixed helper count. Ask for a source-grounded challenge and the smallest authorized discriminating check, with predicted outcomes. Continue independent useful work while helpers investigate; wait only on dependent decisions. Act on supported findings by changing the next experiment or implementation, or explain concretely why they do not apply. A critique alone is not recovery. Skip extra challenges when the cause and fix are established or the work cannot usefully separate. Preserve scope, budgets, ownership and acceptance; proposing a check does not authorize execution.
