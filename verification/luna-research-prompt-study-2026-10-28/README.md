# Luna research-prompt study

> **Portable publication edition.** Private paths and service identifiers have been replaced with portable references. Historical hashes, timestamps, source-byte sizes and validation reports do not validate these transformed files. The [public reproduction guide](../REPRODUCING.md) describes the bundled inputs, supported staged replay and manual research stages; the [current publication manifest](../publication-manifest.json) checks this edition. Published source excerpts support the planning questions, not reconstruction of an entire modified project checkout.

The twelve-run actual-research pilot did **not** qualify either specialized prompt under its frozen cross-domain rule. A separately frozen eight-run continuation experiment found useful source-supported additions after initial discovery, supporting a **source prototype and held-out test** of an optional, bounded follow-up. It did not establish that a mandatory Terra frontier is superior. The held-out full exercise is complete and **inconclusive**: neither planner dispatched the new continuation, and no candidate-only decision-changing mechanism was established. No transfer success, general quality improvement, project outcome, tenfold gain, or savings has been established.

The researchers searched and opened academic and technical sources. This is more than a comparison of proposed plans, but it is not a project campaign: no receiver experiment, project build, regression, architecture change, or runtime qualification was performed. The preceding [decision-routing study](../decision-routing-study-2026-10-28/README.md) compared plans, not actual research findings. All examples here concern prospective decisions and hypothetical targets.

## Design and evidence stages

The [frozen pilot protocol](protocol.json), [common contract](common/contract.md), and [scoring rules](common/scoring.md) compared two domains—pilot-tone acquisition and research/agent workflows—across three instructions with two independent Luna-high runs per domain and arm. A asked for ordinary decision-relevant primary research; B emphasized different approach families and counterevidence; C sought consequential assumptions, failure modes and falsifiers. All received the same domain task and output schema. Each researcher had at most 16 logical web operations, including eight queries and ten opens, a 12-minute target and approximately 1,500 words. These were assignment instructions, not a hard meter; failed operations counted. No number of sources, agents or tokens was required. Ready expert decisions could not wait for discovery or Terra screening.

The [input freeze](input-freeze.json) and [pilot lock](pilot-lock.json) precede four label-blinded Luna verification groups, two Terra domain screens and the [selection](analysis/pilot-selection.json). The shared filesystem and prompt instructions provided protocol separation, not OS isolation; provider identity and a complete independent tool trace were not verified. The original invalid r693 JSON and its serialization-only repair remain in the lock. An initial r733 authorization-context hold was resolved before it researched, with the same standing authorization supplied to all cases.

A submitted finding is **raw** until source checking; a verifier records source identity, accessible content, publication and peer-review status, claim support, applicability and a feasible discriminator. Terra then retains, rejects or leaves it unresolved. Abstract support is limited to what the abstract says; publication metadata alone does not prove peer review or content. A verifier's later access does not retroactively establish what the researcher saw. Expert acceptance is a further, separate stage. Original IDs and rejected or unresolved findings are preserved; multiple reports of the same mechanism are not multiple unique discoveries. See [verification](verification/), [carrier screen](analysis/carrier-screen.md), and [agent screen](analysis/agent-research-screen.md).

## Pilot: negative selection

The frozen rule required each candidate to add an independently supported distinct union finding absent from baseline **in each domain**, with a nonlower mean qualified count, and no material accuracy, omission, timing or unresolved handoff regression. The carrier mean alone defeated both candidates even at unresolved upper bounds; no scoring rule was relaxed. Each case receives credit for its own qualified contributions, so the table is not a count of unique cross-case findings.

Full-content agent B and C means are respectively 4 and 4.5; strict-prefix uncertainty is retained. No individual pilot finding advanced to Sol acceptance merely by being screened. Reported 185 operations across the 12 cases were below the aggregate 192 ceiling, but this does not cure individual deviations: r698 used 20 total and 12 opens; r801 17 total; r612 18 total; r318 10 queries. r698's Baluev full-text open at operation 19 and certain agent findings after the eligible cutoff are unresolved under strict-prefix analysis. [Mechanical diagnostics](analysis/pilot-mechanical-diagnostics.json) preserve case-level operations and packet sizes.

The results still exposed decision-relevant leads with limits. Carrier screens retained comparisons involving finite-record outlier tails, short-window interpolation, phase and lag alternatives, and absent-tone controls. Their ideal single-tone assumptions and abstract/full-text limits do not establish performance for this receiver, its noise, chunks, deadlines or uncertainty intervals. In the agent domain, the [Agentless paper](https://lingming.cs.illinois.edu/publications/fse2025.pdf) describes a staged issue-repair pipeline, while [Chain-of-Verification](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.212/) describes separate verification questions; neither establishes that this kit's research routing improves. [Smit et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/smit24a.html) provide an abstract-level caution about multi-agent debate under their studied conditions, not a universal prohibition. See the screens for source locators and unknown publication statuses.

## Separately frozen continuation

Following the negative pilot, the [continuation protocol](continuation/protocol.json) reused all four baseline cases as common seeds, without selecting the best seed. Eight fresh Luna-high researchers ran paired continuations: generic evidence-driven follow-up versus a Terra-produced, seed-linked frontier with no new external research. Each pair had equal Luna ceilings; the frontier additionally incurred Terra preparation. The seed-only comparison is an **extra research opportunity**, not an equal-cost comparison. Research, verification and screening were locked in sequence: [research](continuation/research-lock.json), [verification](continuation/verification-lock.json), [screens](continuation/screen-lock.json). Four independent verification groups and two Terra screens applied the original support rules and strict prefixes.

| Domain / seed | Generic qualified lower–upper | Frontier qualified lower–upper |
|---|---:|---:|
| Carrier s1 | n641 2–2 | n273 1–1 |
| Carrier s2 | n532 0–1 | n685 1–1 |
| Agent s3 | n836 1–1 | n154 1–2 |
| Agent s4 | n309 1–1 | n494 1–2 |

Per-domain means were carrier generic 1–1.5 versus frontier 1, and agent generic 1 versus frontier 1–2. A shared mechanism can occur in both cases, so these per-case counts are not a deduplicated union. The frozen frontier-superiority condition was not met; decisive unresolved items remain. The narrower frozen generic fallback was met by at least one independently supported incremental contribution in each domain, with no established critical regression. This supports a provisional optional continuation, not a claim that generic beats frontier or that a second pass saves effort. The [selection record](continuation/analysis/selection.json) and [carrier](continuation/analysis/carrier-screen.md) and [agent](continuation/analysis/agent-screen.md) screens preserve per-finding stages and deductions.

The continuation contributed 73 self-reported logical operations. n273 exceeded the 12-minute target by about 20 seconds; n154 and n494 verifiers exceeded their dedicated eight-operation limits, and the s4 verifier exceeded its stated ten-open limit. Screens use eligible prefixes and do not credit the excess suffix. n641 and n494 raw reports use seed IDs as their case IDs; assigned folder and seed identities are retained separately. The GNSS staged-dwell analogy remains unresolved for carrier transfer. In the agent domain a value-of-information citation has a mismatched title/identity and unsupported details, and CodeRAG's compound path/pruning claim remains unresolved. CoBiT's peer-review status was corrected to unknown; its supported method is not evidence of repository-architecture benefit.

For concrete source-backed examples, Sol independently inspected [So and Chan's institutional record](https://scholars.cityu.edu.hk/en/publications/short-time-frequency-estimation-of-a-real-sinusoid/): the abstract supports short-record real-sinusoid periodogram bias and correction controls, and the institution classifies the journal article as peer reviewed. The feasible next question is whether a matched phase/aperture and image-correction comparison matters for these captures; the article does not establish receiver performance. Sol also opened [RepoCoder](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.151.pdf), §§1–2 and Table 2: candidate-conditioned retrieval is studied for repository code completion, suggesting a bounded follow-up retrieval hypothesis. Its limitations at printed p. 2479 include low duplication, unstable later iterations and added latency. These are source-backed hypotheses, not proof of benefit for smaller-model architecture research. Other individual findings remain expert-unassessed. Sol's narrow review required a locator correction and an added caveat, with no blocking factual follow-up for that decision; it did not measure general expert reconstruction savings. See [candidate review](implementation/candidate-review.md).

## Source prototype and held-out transfer

The reviewed source prototype changes existing research guidance in `policy.md`, `agents/luna_scout.toml` and `README.md`: when a consequential evidence gap can change a decision, a coordinator may dispatch bounded incremental primary-source research with locators, applicability, counterevidence, stop conditions and no filler. It keeps ready expert judgment immediate, Terra screening optional, direct original-source access and independent acceptance. It creates no role, scheduler, quota, or mandatory research stage. The source-before snapshots and [first prototype diff](implementation/first-prototype.diff) record the initial change; subsequent [wording repairs](implementation/wording-repairs.json) corrected the held-out status and removed the runtime study link, which is absent from the installation bundle. See the actual current files for the final prototype, rather than treating the first diff as final.

The source HEAD was `947ed25d4058e405ecf45d6956671f6fecb12026`, with [pre-existing dirty work](source-baseline.json) preserved. Two temporary homes tested baseline and candidate installation: the 21-file bundles matched their corresponding source bytes, role selectors and sentinel primary model were preserved, and repeat previews reported no changes. Existing installer and distribution tests passed 48 + 4; the four distribution checks passed again after the final wording repairs. These are temporary installation checks, not a global deployment or proof that already-running native roles refreshed. See [initial checks](implementation/installation-checks.json), [final temporary-install checks](implementation/final-installation-checks.json), and the [final source-prototype diff](implementation/final-prototype.diff).

The [held-out protocol](common/transfer-protocol.md) was frozen before pilot results. It used a fresh research objective over a previously known regression scenario and source packet, held out from the researchers but not wholly unseen to the coordinator. The baseline p204 was the **current Luna-heavy kit immediately before this request**, not an older pre-tenfold policy. Candidate p517 used the source prototype. Both independent Terra planners had the same maxima and initially selected four Luna scouts, but chose 64 versus 32 research-operation budgets. Both assigned all 13 source units. Their admitted Luna assignments performed actual research, then the planners received identical hypothetical developments; neither requested an additional research assignment. No project campaign was run.

Another candidate scout reported an open without a locator. A baseline scout's inventory command printed filenames of unassigned artifacts, although it reported not opening or using them; isolation was procedural and no complete independent tool trace establishes otherwise. Aggregate counts do not cure these per-case deviations. See [deviations](transfer/analysis/initial-deviations.json) and [mechanical summary](transfer/analysis/mechanical-summary.json).

Independent [Sol review](transfer/analysis/sol-review.md) checked original sources and all eight reports, consumers and configuration. It preserved the original acceptance and found **zero established candidate-only decision-changing mechanisms** after normalizing overlapping questions, so the full success criterion was not met. In particular, candidate [Memon](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~atif/papers/MemonASE2003-abstract.html) and [Jiang](https://scholars.cityu.edu.hk/en/publications/a-systematic-study-on-factors-impacting-gui-traversal-based-test-/) abstracts gave stronger verified rationale for GUI observation choices, but the relevant state and consumer checks already appeared in baseline and the scenario. That is not proof of universal absence of benefit. A separate [erratum](transfer/analysis/reviewer-corrections.json) corrects p517's R02 help coverage: ordinary successful redirection is not explicitly asserted, while a failing `system-fixture/dev/full` sink is conditionally covered. The published packet retains the decision and erratum, with coordinates and identifiers transformed.

The next hypothetical discriminator is to capture actual installed artifacts, provider and test inventory alongside a same-consumer event-to-state-to-presentation trace, preserving the failing artifact and comparing a direct path where feasible. Missing identities and traces remain unresolved; literature does not diagnose the project. [Astra's strategic review](transfer/analysis/astra-review.md) supports retaining optional wording as a source clarification, not an allocation, causal, savings or adoption claim, and recommends no automatic tuning. A later authorized causal study would need a naturally occurring evidence-resolvable gap and a matched continue-versus-stop comparison that measures incremental decision value and total verification burden without delaying a ready decision. This single stochastic planner pair, differing chosen budgets, imperfect operation provenance and unused second pass cannot establish transfer or generalization. The prototype remains source-only; deployment and adoption were not accepted.

Original administration and review records retain their historical scope.
