# Frozen evaluation contract
Unit: a stable original case_id/finding_id is a submitted claim with a decision implication, source support, applicability and feasible discriminator. A decision-useful finding changes a plausible next decision, rejects a consequential assumption or supplies a feasible discriminating check under the given constraints. A different citation/name for the same mechanism is not another finding. Deduplicate within each report while retaining every origin ID; shared discoveries earn credit in each original case. Never fill one arm's gap with another's finding. Verifier-discovered additions/corrections are separate, never retroactive researcher credit.

Code each original finding: discovered; source content accessible (full/abstract/metadata/no); publication status verified (yes/no/unknown with venue evidence); claim support (direct full / abstract-limited / unsupported / unverifiable); task applicability (adequately mapped / missing assumption / inapplicable / unresolved); alternative mechanism or consequential caveat; next discriminator feasible (yes/no/uncertain); qualified decision-useful (yes/no/unresolved with reason). Abstract-limited support qualifies only for claims actually supported by that abstract and with appropriately limited implications. Publication unknown does not make a supported useful finding false, and peer review alone does not establish applicability. Count peer-reviewed primary sources separately from other primary evidence. Incomplete verification is unresolved, not false; publish unresolved lower/upper count bounds.

Stage matrix retains original IDs through independent verification, Terra retained/rejected/unresolved with reasons, and expert reviewed useful/defective/not individually assessed. Preserve consequential rejected candidates and dissent. Do not silently relabel expert-unassessed as accepted. Report omissions, erroneous publication labels, unsupported mathematical/benchmark transfer, proposed gate delay, duplication and report/packet lengths separately.

Comparison: publish all 12 case scores. For each domain/arm report (a) each replicate's qualified count and unresolved count, (b) mean qualified count over the two runs, (c) deduplicated union with original case IDs, and (d) distinct viable mechanisms, caveats and discriminators. Counts are descriptive, not independent statistical samples. Source-verification coverage and unknown upper bounds must accompany comparisons.

Provisional selection rule: compared with A, a candidate must have in EACH domain at least one independently supported distinct decision-useful union finding absent from A's union AND a mean qualified per-run count at least as high as A. It must have no material accuracy/critical-omission/gate-timing regression and no additional unresolved handoff defect or expert-required factual reconstruction. Decisive unverified claims make selection inconclusive. If both qualify, prefer fewer observed handoff defects/factual follow-ups, then more cross-domain verified distinct gains; tie means no unique winner and retain/report both rather than pick on cost. If neither qualifies, revise with disclosed separate exploratory tests or retain baseline; do not relax this frozen rule. Pilot success is provisional; implementation acceptance needs held-out transfer, source/distribution checks and independent review. No tenfold or savings result is presumed.
