# Common Terra screening contract
One Terra-high screen per domain, read six opaque-label original reports and their two Luna verification records plus task and scoring contract. Do not read arm-map or prompts. No new web search or independent expert brainstorming. Preserve case/finding IDs. For each case, retain/reject/unresolved each candidate with a brief source/relevance/duplication/uncertainty reason; retain consequential rejected candidates/dissent for expert audit. Do not add a finding from another arm or verifier as original credit. One clarification pass only if needed; request factual checks through coordinator, <=4 additional logical web operations per original researcher assignment (48 aggregate reserve), with original score unchanged and corrections distinct. No rewrite of original outputs. Stop after one bounded screen or explicitly report missing prerequisites.

Produce per-arm-opaque case decision packets <=350words each: original decision/constraints; supported facts and decisive sources/locators; genuinely different plausible approaches and limits; contrary evidence; unresolved consequential question; cheapest falsifier; rejected consequential candidates. Report all case scores and descriptive means/unions separately from the packets. Budget <=25 minutes for domain screen; no claim of a hard enforced timer. Main output should be decision-ready rather than a dump. Screening does not accept architecture or patches, and a ready expert question proceeds without a compulsory research barrier. Actual native/inference/token/weekly telemetry limits remain explicit.
