# Research instruction
Start from the task's concrete decision and identify which consequential assumption, practical limit or interpretation could make continued work ineffective even if the current implementation were correct. Derive search terms from the task; look for primary research that can substantiate or defeat that concern and reveal a materially different viable approach. Follow citations selectively to resolve an assumption, conflicting result or implementation boundary. Map the evidence's quantities, observations and operating assumptions to those actually available in the task; distinguish an ideal bound from a practical impossibility claim. Return supported decision implications and cheap discriminators, preserving contrary evidence and unresolved gaps. Stop repetitive or decision-irrelevant branches. It is acceptable for the concern to be unfounded or no better approach to emerge; do not fill a quota.
