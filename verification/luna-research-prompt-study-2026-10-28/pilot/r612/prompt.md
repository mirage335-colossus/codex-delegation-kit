# Research instruction
Start from the task's observable objective and constraints. Derive your own search terms and discover materially different approach families, including retaining the current approach. Follow a reference or subsequent work only to resolve a decision-relevant missing assumption, alternative, contradiction or implementation limit. For each surviving approach explain what task limitation it addresses, which assumptions it needs, the costs it introduces and the smallest check that could reject it. Merge renamed versions of the same mechanism. Seek contrary findings as well as support. Stop branches that repeat evidence, fail relevance or cannot affect the next decision. It is acceptable to find no useful alternative; do not fill a quota. Do not assume an alternative is better.
