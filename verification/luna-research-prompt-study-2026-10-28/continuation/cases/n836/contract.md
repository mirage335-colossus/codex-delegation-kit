# Evidence-driven continuation contract
This is a separate actual-research experiment after a failed standalone-prompt pilot. The common discovery packet is frozen source-grounded work produced by Luna and checked without expensive expert-seeded algorithms. You receive a task, discovery.json, source-checks.json and source excerpts. Use these as starting evidence, not new contributions. Do not seek previous pilot scores, other continuations or hidden arms. The intervention is generic continuation versus continuation plus a Terra evidence-gap map; it is a workflow bundle, not a prompt-only or total-cost-controlled comparison.

Do bounded additional academic primary-source research. Find incremental decision-useful evidence beyond the packet: a supported new feasible mechanism, resolved consequential uncertainty, corrected assumption, or usable new discriminator. Another citation or rewording is not new. You may find no worthwhile addition. Prefer relevant peer-reviewed original sources; verify publication and access status, cite exact accessible locators, preserve preprint/unknown/abstract-only limits. Existing evidence still matters: final handoff must retain its breadth/requirements and disclose unresolved issues. Do not overstate ideal bounds or cross-model benchmarks.

Budget: <=16 total logical web operations, <=8 search queries and <=10 source opens. Each batched query/URL/find/click/screenshot counts separately, failed attempts too. Before every batch, count its operations and remaining total and per-kind allowance; reduce the batch to fit. Report actual use and overshoots; this prompt is not hard enforcement. Target <=12min; stop when sufficient/low-value or at first limit. No retrieval via shell, model/provider changes or access workarounds. No code/project campaigns, project/global edits, nested agents or final architecture acceptance.

Write only own report.json in the provided original pilot schema, with extra incremental_vs_seed for each finding (new evidence and which seed claim/gap it changes). At most6top additions, <=1500words, plus retained_seed_summary <=200words; keep original sources/status/provenance and search failures. No desired numerical result, paper count, token quota or obligation to find a better approach. Ready consequential judgment may proceed without waiting for research.

Assign sequential operation IDs and record source first-content/status-check operation IDs. Each finding must list support_operation_ids. Distinguish seed support from new retrieval. Where in-budget support cannot be separated from later content, strict-prefix status is unresolved and earns no lower-bound credit; do not backfill provenance from memory. Failed/batched operations still count.
