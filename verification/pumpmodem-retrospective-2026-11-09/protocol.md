# pumpModem retrospective exercise protocol

This local study answers whether additional parallel GPT-6 Luna review could expose avoidable decisions in the chat **Speed up Robust CPU throughput estimates** before compilation or testing forced correction. It does not change pumpModem, the delegation policy, model pins or installed configuration. Original evidence remains in its existing local locations. No publication is requested.

## Method fixed before exercise responses

Use reconstructed historical decision packets, with source revision and cutoff recorded for each. Packet preparers know later outcomes; exercise participants receive only the packet, a review lens and this common task. They must not inspect later source, historical conversations, answer keys, other responses or current documentation. This is instruction-based blinding in a shared filesystem, not access-control isolation. Record any known leakage.

For each substantial case, obtain three independent Luna-high responses with the same packet and equal output/time limits: one general engineering reviewer, one focused on architecture and quantitative feasibility, and one focused on the cheapest executable falsifier. Compare the generalist response with the union of the three. This compares additional work and differentiated prompts as well as parallelism; it cannot isolate the causal effect of scheduling or model reliability. Retain good or reasonably tested approaches as negative controls. Do not manufacture cases to fill a quota.

Each participant returns at most three concrete concerns, each with a mechanism, packet citation, predicted observable result, a small executable check and the action the result would change. They also identify a decision worth preserving. No generic warning receives detection credit.

## Scoring

- **Specific supported detection:** predicts the actual mechanism or a decisive check that would expose it, using available predecision facts. Establish historical applicability separately.
- **Useful gate:** proposes a relevant discriminating check but does not identify the actual failure. This is useful process advice, not an early bug detection.
- **Partial:** identifies the affected area or risk but omits the mechanism or runnable discriminator.
- **Miss:** does not flag a consequential supported issue within its packet scope.
- **False alarm:** asserts an actual defect contradicted by evidence or recommends rejecting an approach that the available evidence supports. An honestly conditional hypothesis remains unverified rather than automatically false.
- **Duplicate:** repeats another participant's mechanism without adding a discriminating observation.
- **Untestable or unresolved:** evidence or required environment is missing; no success credit.

Classify historical cases as architectural mistake, implementation/integration bug, measurement/provenance error, setup/resource issue, or reasonable negative experiment. Separate chronological association from causal proof. Do not credit current-source behavior to a historical revision, kernel gains to complete receiver gains, forecast accuracy to real-time capacity, or thread counts to productive same-stream utilization.

## Executable checks and resource limits

Use small local reproductions in owned scratch paths or this study's checks directory. Historical and current pumpModem checkouts remain read-only. No cloud resources, credentials, production changes or full receiver campaign. Compile actual affected code or invoke actual helpers when feasible; label mathematical reductions and reimplementations. Serialize timing-sensitive work and keep numerical diagnostics separate from throughput qualification. Preserve original failures, setup failures, exact commands and source identity.

Record requested model and effort, available runtime telemetry, wall intervals and missing usage. Agent counts or token totals do not establish savings. The user-provided weekly usage index (Luna 0.1, Terra 1, Sol approximately 10, Astra approximately 20) is unitless and is not a conversion to dollars or measured allowance consumption.

## Arbitration and completion

Screen predictions against decisive original evidence, retain disagreements and misses, then ask Astra to arbitrate the historical judgments and the simplest better workflow. Astra separately reviews whether this retrospective can conclude or whether feasible required investigation remains. The deliverable includes chronology, case studies, exercise results, actual checks, limitations and arbitration.
