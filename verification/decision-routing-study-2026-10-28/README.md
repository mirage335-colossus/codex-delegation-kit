Follow the [public reproduction guide](../REPRODUCING.md) to stage a new independent run using the bundled inputs; the [current publication manifest](../publication-manifest.json) checks this edition.

The locked plans show more explicit Luna investigation in these two examples, with uncertain distinct-question gains and no evidence of a tenfold increase: carrier initially 4 versus 5 primary Luna questions, regression 10 versus 10–11 under the reviewer’s grouping sensitivity. ## Starting point and fixed objective

The [common contract](common/contract.md) gave both arms the same six reasoning slots including coordinator and reviewers, at most four future test processes, eight CPUs, 16 GiB, a shared configured build, and a first-two-active-hours horizon followed by two developments. Shared writers and nested outputs must be serialized. Settings were fixed at Terra high, Luna high, Sol ultra and Astra medium. All consequential architectural, algorithmic or measurement-contract decisions require Sol or authorized Astra review before substantial dependent implementation; independent patch review, original failure history, one evidence-backed local repair and authorization boundaries remain. Astra is timely for strategic uncertainty, contradictions or costly wrong turns. Human clarification is early when material, and silence is not permission. These are planning conditions, not observed resources or permission to execute.

[Carrier](common/carrier/scenario.md) asks whether generated-truth captures can produce useful frequency-only estimates soon after tone onset across offsets −12, −3, +3 and +12 Hz, signal levels, noise, payload/metadata activity and chunk schedules. Its deliberately hypothetical targets include at least 95% of tone-present captures publishing an estimate within 0.10 Hz by six seconds, truth coverage for every finite interval, and no accepted absent-tone publication. Report timing, error, uncertainty, nonpublication and resources; preserve frequency, phase, presence, endpoint and payload contracts. Source snapshots H01–H12 are current code/test/document excerpts, not runtime results. The named source revision was dirty at packet preparation; its stated baseline was preserved.

[Regression](common/regression/scenario.md) starts with eight invented reports spanning store, CLI, provider, GUI, native task, browser, scrolling and editor behavior. The acceptance rubric requires each report classified with source/consumer paths, focused fixture limits and matched causal discriminator, plus affected consumers across core/provider, executable or GUI, and build/retained-input layers. Keep setup failures, untested work, reproduced failures and rejected hypotheses distinct; do not weaken assertions. The source snapshot was clean at packet preparation. Registrations are source facts, not passed tests; R09–R13 are neighboring units, not assumed incidents. Neither packet establishes a diagnosis or authorizes project tests, builds, edits or hardware work.

## What was compared and how

[A](common/policy-A.md) reconstructs a preceding mixed-quality approach; it is not a historical bill or verbatim prior deployment. It permits broad Luna discovery when justified, ordinary Terra synthesis/checking and direct expert inspection. [B](common/policy-B.md) retains those obligations and adds a preference to proactively seek substantially more useful independent Luna discovery, including alternative approaches and neighboring evidence, with worthwhile Terra screening and compact expert handoffs. The tenfold preference is conditional on genuinely distinct useful work and a comparable baseline; it is neither a token floor nor an agent quota. Screening cannot block a ready strategic decision or replace review. Quality gates and source packets were the same.

The [protocol](protocol.json) froze contracts, sources and fabricated events before four independent Terra-high planners, one per arm/scenario. [Preparation corrections](preparation-changes.json) were made before planning, including source-unit count, neutral regression E2 and carrier event/denominator wording. Initial submissions were locked together, E1 released identically and its four responses locked, then E2 released identically and its four responses locked. See [initial lock](locked/initial-lock.json), [E1 release](locked/E1-release.json), [E1 lock](locked/E1-lock.json), [E2 release](locked/E2-release.json), [E2 lock](locked/E2-lock.json), and the [published representations of the historically locked plans](locked/). Only after this did separate coders derive questions and usage; no cost or token calculation informed the original decisions. [Arm map](analysis/arm-map.json): carrier A=p17, B=p43; regression A=p61, B=p29.

The separately produced [independent validation](analysis/independent-validation.json) reports 1,041 passing hash, equality, lock-order, structural and arithmetic checks with no failures. It does not establish provider identity, calibrate the estimates, or prove the planners complied with their reading boundary. The represented decisions retain their substance; private coordinates and identifiers have been transformed for this edition.

An additional [141 correction checks](analysis/correction-validation.json) independently verify the revised accounting and explicit assigned source scope. These checks verify consistency; they do not measure campaign performance.

Planners were instructed to read only their assigned materials and use no other arm or unreleased event. This was protocol blinding in a shared filesystem, not OS isolation; hidden prompt assembly and provider identity were not independently audited. Coders treated participant labels as opaque, but model and routing language could reveal arm: coding was only partially blind. There is one planner per cell, no random-effects estimate and no actual downstream campaign. A source unit explicitly assigned is planned scope, not proof of a read, test coverage or finding. A qualified candidate question is decision-relevant, feasible and nonredundant under the rubric, not an accepted answer.

## Sequential developments and expert work

All developments are fabricated messages, not observed project runs. Carrier [E1 and E2](common/carrier/developments.json) describe, respectively, four confounded mixed capture summaries including an interval miss, then a separate twelve-capture set with ten publications by eight seconds, three interval misses among those ten and two without publication by ten seconds. Neither has raw PCM, full provenance or controlled causal evidence. Both plans seek records and a matched chunk comparison; E2 activates bounded Astra advice, keeps the original six-second/error/coverage targets and considers longer windows as diagnostics rather than replacement acceptance. Do not pool cohorts, infer population rates or equate final eight-second errors with six-second acquisition.

Regression [E1 and E2](common/regression/developments.json) give an uncontrolled store observation across a rebuild and unmarked provider success, then a direct-GUI pass alongside installed-consumer stale state with differing provider and test inventories. Both plans preserve missing identity and original uncertainties, request matched traces and activate Astra for the cross-component branch. Matching declared revision and manifest is not proof of runtime linkage or a cause. The strategic advice is retained even where Luna investigates factual boundaries; Sol still owns consequential review and independent artifact review. Downstream expert processing was not measured: Terra screening could reduce Sol reading or add reconstruction and handoff burden. Astra remains in both arms; no reduction in strategic quality or improvement in recognizing redesign was observed, and neither arm selected an actual redesign. Expert handoffs retain original requirements, decisive source references, alternatives, dissent, provenance limits and unresolved questions, with direct source access. No screen is an approval or mandatory wait.

Both arms reject undirected literature surveys or repeated mapping once evidence is sufficient, speculative repairs and broad matrices. Carrier excludes phase reconstruction, hardware qualification and algorithm changes as substitutes for the frequency question; regression excludes an unrelated full regression or release campaign and retained-kernel operations. B's broader discovery preference did not entail treating all neighboring helpers as incidents. No project research campaign or project execution was performed; official API pricing was browsed for this study.
