   96 ## Fixed framing and physical completion
   97 
   98 Received header lengths must not drive modem boundaries, memory allocation,
   99 acquisition or message completion. Keep fixed local geometry and bounded
  100 storage while receiving. Corrected source areas stay in bounded RAM (no temporary disk files), but all
  101 source decoding, decompression and attachment-prefix interpretation wait until
  102 physical completion. Even a complete codec stream or an underfilled interval
  103 cannot finish reception early. After physical completion, the existing source
  104 codec and bounded attachment prefix may be interpreted under local quotas;
  105 this does not authorize introducing a packet-length parser.
  106 
  107 The only physical end is observed absence of admitted symbols: score complete
  108 symbols, and end after consecutive failed durations cover at least six seconds.
  109 For a fixed duration `T`, this requires `ceil(6/T)` complete failed symbols.
  110 At four hours per symbol, six seconds of silence within the next symbol is not
  111 enough; one fully observed absent four-hour symbol is. EOF, cancellation, quotas,
  112 receiver replacement, source validity, FEC and MAC outcomes never manufacture
  113 physical completion. Simulation and recorded audio obey the same rule.
  114

  116 window until that window has actually been scored. Wall-clock age alone cannot
  117 discard unprocessed samples when decoding or simulation runs slowly. Preserve
