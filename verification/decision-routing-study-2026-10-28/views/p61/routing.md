# Routing policy — reconstruction of preceding approach
This reconstruction retains the prior mixed-quality strategy and all common quality gates. It is not a verbatim historical deployment.
Delegate useful separable evidence gathering, routine diagnostics, settled implementation, and targeted regressions to Luna when task fit, independence and expected progress justify the assignment. Broad source/caller/test mapping and relevant external research are available to Luna when useful; do not restrict it to trivial work. The Terra coordinator handles ordinary engineering/synthesis and may check findings as needed. Sol/Astra retain the common consequential judgment and review responsibilities and may directly inspect focused sources/evidence. Reuse supported findings and ask for bounded missing factual work instead of repeating whole investigations. Dispatch ready independent work before waiting, within shared resources; avoid redundant or filler work. This policy has no additional preference to proactively seek a greatly expanded Luna work allocation and no dedicated Terra evidence-screening stage. It does not prohibit either broad Luna investigation or ordinary Terra checking when independently justified by the task.
