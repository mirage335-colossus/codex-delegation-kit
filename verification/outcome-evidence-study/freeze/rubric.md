# Pre-dispatch evaluation
Score each arm independently: (1) discovers a material omitted behavior/condition, (2) distinguishes evidence for distinct purposes where relevant, (3) initiates and executes a meaningful check, implementation or defensible bounded estimate, (4) correctly scopes conclusions and preserves failed checks, (5) honors assignment limits and avoids unnecessary expansion. For receiver exercise, assess end-to-end work, search/tracking workload, actual throughput versus a regression proxy; do not require a production receiver from the synthetic fixture. For transfer, assess the actual decision and end-to-end conditions defined by the case. For narrow control, assess completion without gratuitous expansion. Record concrete artifacts and commands; explanation alone is not execution. Unknowns are unknown, not failures. Match common context, role, tools, and resource allowance; candidate adds only candidate.txt. Results are exploratory due to synthetic cases, one trial per arm and shared filesystem; no assertion of randomization or full blinding.
